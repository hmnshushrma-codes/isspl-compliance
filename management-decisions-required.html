1. Scope & Framework Decisions

#Decision RequiredOptions / GuidanceAffected DocumentsPriorityDecision
1 SOC 2 Trust Services Categories in scope Security is mandatory. Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity, and Privacy are optional. Recommend including Availability and Confidentiality if customer SLAs or data handling agreements exist. RS-SOC2-001, RS-SOC2-003, RS-SOC2-004 Critical [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS]
2 SOC 2 Type I or Type II Type I: design/implementation at a point in time. Type II: design + operating effectiveness over a period (3–12 months). Type II is more valuable for customer trust. RS-SOC2-001, evidence collection timeline Critical [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS]
3 SOC 1 applicability Required only if Ramdoot Solutions' services affect customer Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR). If services are purely software/SaaS without financial transaction processing, SOC 1 may not be applicable. RS-SOC1-001, RS-SOC1-003 High [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS]
4 Target audit period For SOC 2 Type II: 3 months (minimum), 6 months (common first audit), or 12 months (standard). Evidence collection must begin before the audit period starts. All evidence documents, RS-EV-REQ-001 Critical [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS]
5 System boundaries Confirm which systems are in scope: CertFlow web app, API, database, MinIO, PDF parser, CI/CD, email service, DNS/CDN. Exclude any components not relevant to services provided to customers. RS-SOC2-002, RS-SOC2-004 High [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS]
