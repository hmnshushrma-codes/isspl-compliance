1. Purpose and Scope

This Evidence Register provides a comprehensive mapping of all evidence artifacts required to demonstrate Ramdoot Solutions' compliance with the AICPA SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (TSC 2017). It serves as the single source of truth for identifying, tracking, and managing evidence items that support the organization's SOC 2 Type II examination.

The register covers the following Trust Services Categories:

  • Common Criteria (CC1–CC9): Security (applicable to all SOC 2 engagements)
  • Availability (A1): System availability commitments
  • Confidentiality (C1): Information designated as confidential

Note: Each evidence item is assigned a unique identifier (RS-EV-xxx) and mapped to the corresponding SOC 2 criteria, related policies, and controls. Evidence owners are responsible for maintaining current evidence and ensuring timely collection per the defined frequency.

2. How to Use This Register

This register should be used as follows:

  • Evidence Owners — Identify your assigned evidence items, ensure artifacts are current, and upload to the designated storage location.
  • Compliance Team — Track evidence collection progress, coordinate with owners, and verify completeness prior to the audit period.
  • External Auditors — Use the storage location links to access evidence artifacts during the SOC 2 examination.
  • Management — Monitor overall readiness via the summary statistics section.
