1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document maps each AICPA Trust Services Criteria (TSC) point to the controls, policies, and evidence maintained by Ramdoot Solutions. It serves as the primary internal reference for demonstrating how the organization addresses the Common Criteria and, where elected, the additional Trust Services Categories of Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity, and Privacy.

1.2 Scope

The scope of this document covers all Ramdoot Solutions information systems, infrastructure, personnel, policies, and processes that support the delivery of services to clients. This includes production environments, development and staging environments where they impact production data, corporate IT systems, and third-party services integrated into the service delivery chain.

1.3 How to Read This Document

Each criterion section contains the following subsections:

  • Criteria Reference & Requirement: The official TSC reference number and a summary of the requirement.
  • Points of Focus: Key areas that auditors and management should consider (summarized, not verbatim).
  • Ramdoot Solutions Control Response: How the organization addresses the criterion.
  • Related Policies: Links to supporting policy and procedure documents.
  • Required Evidence: Artifacts that demonstrate the control is operating effectively.
  • Status: Current implementation status indicated by a badge.

Status Badges Legend:

Implemented — Control is in place and operating effectively.
Partial — Control exists but requires further work.
Draft — Control is planned or documented but not yet deployed.
Required — Control is needed but not yet started.

2. CC1 — Control Environment

CC1.1 — Commitment to Integrity and Ethical Values

Criteria Requirement

The entity demonstrates a commitment to integrity and ethical values. Management sets the tone at the top, establishing standards of conduct that are understood and followed throughout the organization.

Points of Focus

  • Existence of a Code of Conduct or Code of Ethics applicable to all personnel
  • Communication and enforcement of ethical standards, including disciplinary measures for violations
  • Assessment of adherence to standards of conduct as part of performance evaluations
  • Processes for addressing deviations from established standards in a timely manner

Ramdoot Solutions Control Response

Ramdoot Solutions shall maintain a formal Code of Conduct that articulates expected ethical behaviour for all employees, contractors, and third-party personnel. The Code of Conduct shall be provided to every new joiner during onboarding and acknowledged in writing. Management shall reinforce ethical expectations through periodic communications and shall include adherence to ethical standards as a factor in annual performance reviews. Violations shall be investigated and addressed through the organization's disciplinary process, up to and including termination.

Related Policies

  • Code of Conduct (RS-GOV-001)
  • Disciplinary Policy (RS-GOV-004)
  • Information Security Policy (RS-SEC-001)

Required Evidence

  • Signed Code of Conduct acknowledgement forms for all current employees
  • Records of annual ethics training or awareness communications
  • Evidence of disciplinary action taken for code violations (redacted as appropriate)
  • Performance review templates showing ethical conduct as an evaluation criterion

CC3.2 — Risk Identification and Analysis

Criteria Requirement

The entity identifies risks to the achievement of its objectives across the entity and analyzes risks as a basis for determining how the risks should be managed.

Points of Focus

  • Comprehensive risk identification covering internal and external threats
  • Risk analysis methodology including likelihood and impact assessment
  • Consideration of risks at different levels: entity, department, process, and system

Risks shall be categorized by domain (e.g. technology, operational, compliance, reputational) and prioritized for treatment based on the organization's risk appetite.

CC4.2 — Deficiency Communication

Criteria Requirement

The entity evaluates and communicates internal control deficiencies in a timely manner to those parties responsible for taking corrective action, including senior management and the board of directors as appropriate.

Points of Focus

  • Defined process for evaluating the severity of identified control deficiencies
  • Timely communication of deficiencies to appropriate levels of management
  • Tracking of remediation actions and verification of closure

Ramdoot Solutions Control Response

Ramdoot Solutions shall maintain a formal deficiency tracking process. All deficiencies shall be logged in a corrective action tracker with assigned owners, target remediation dates, and status updates. Deficiencies that remain open beyond their target date shall be escalated to the next management level.

7. CC6 — Logical and Physical Access

CC6.1 — Logical Access Security

Criteria Requirement

The entity implements logical access security software, infrastructure, and architectures over protected information assets to protect them from security events.

Points of Focus

  • Identification and authentication mechanisms for all users and system accounts
  • Network security architecture including segmentation, firewalls, and intrusion detection
  • Encryption of data at rest and in transit
  • Protection of authentication credentials

Ramdoot Solutions Control Response

Ramdoot Solutions shall implement layered logical access controls across all systems. All users shall be uniquely identified and authenticated using strong authentication mechanisms, including multi-factor authentication (MFA) for access to production systems and administrative consoles. Network security shall be enforced through firewalls, network segmentation, and intrusion detection/prevention systems. Data shall be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent. Authentication credentials shall be stored using industry-standard hashing algorithms and shall never be stored in plaintext.

Related Policies

  • Access Control Policy (RS-SEC-002)
  • Encryption Policy (RS-SEC-003)
  • Network Security Policy (RS-SEC-005)

Required Evidence

  • MFA configuration records for production and administrative access
  • Firewall rule sets and network architecture diagrams
  • TLS certificate inventory and configuration scan results
  • Encryption key management records

Status

CC6.3 — Role-Based Access

Criteria Requirement

The entity authorizes, modifies, or removes access to data, software, functions, and other protected information assets based on roles, responsibilities, or the system design and changes, giving consideration to the concepts of least privilege and segregation of duties.

Points of Focus

  • Role-based access control (RBAC) implementation across all critical systems
  • Access modification processes triggered by role changes (transfers, promotions)
  • Periodic access reviews to ensure access remains appropriate
  • Segregation of conflicting duties enforced through access controls

Ramdoot Solutions Control Response

Ramdoot Solutions shall implement role-based access control across all production systems and business applications. Access roles shall be defined based on job functions, and access shall be granted strictly in accordance with the principle of least privilege. Access shall be modified promptly upon changes in role or responsibility. Quarterly access reviews shall be conducted by system owners to verify that all active user accounts and their associated permissions remain appropriate. Segregation of duties shall be enforced through role design, preventing any single individual from controlling all aspects of a critical process.

Related Policies

  • Access Control Policy (RS-SEC-002)
  • User Provisioning Procedure (RS-SEC-013)

Required Evidence

  • RBAC role definitions and permission matrices
  • Quarterly access review reports
  • Access modification tickets for role changes
  • Segregation of duties conflict analysis
