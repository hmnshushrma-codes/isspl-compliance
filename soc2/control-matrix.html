1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This SOC 2 Control Matrix enumerates the internal controls that Ramdoot Solutions maintains (or intends to implement) in alignment with the AICPA Trust Services Criteria (TSC). The matrix serves as the central reference for gap analysis, remediation tracking, and audit readiness assessment. Each control is mapped to one or more TSC criteria, with clear ownership, frequency, evidence requirements, and implementation status.

1.2 Scope

This matrix covers the Common Criteria (CC1 through CC9) of the Security trust services category. Controls are organized into nine families corresponding to the CC groupings. The scope encompasses all information systems, processes, personnel, and third-party relationships that support Ramdoot Solutions' service delivery.

Note: Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy criteria may be addressed in supplemental matrices as Ramdoot Solutions expands its compliance program.

1.3 Methodology

Each control in this matrix has been defined using the following approach:

  • TSC Mapping: Direct reference to the applicable Trust Services Criteria point(s).
  • Control Design: Description of the control's intended design, including operational detail sufficient for an auditor to understand the mechanism.
  • Evidence Expectations: Specific artifacts and records that must be produced to demonstrate operating effectiveness.
  • Status Assessment: Honest assessment of current implementation maturity — no control is marked as fully implemented without independent verification.

RS-IAM-001 CC6.1 Identity Lifecycle Management Manage the complete lifecycle of user identities from provisioning through deprovisioning. Ramdoot Solutions maintains an identity lifecycle management process that governs: (a) account provisioning — new accounts are created only upon receipt of an approved access request from the employee's manager, specifying role-based access requirements; (b) account modification — role changes trigger a review of existing access, with unnecessary privileges removed within 5 business days; (c) account deprovisioning — upon employee termination or contractor end-date, all accounts are disabled within 24 hours and deleted within 30 days, triggered by HR notification to IT; (d) account inventory — a complete list of all user accounts across systems is maintained and reconciled quarterly against the HR active employee roster; and (e) orphan account detection — quarterly automated or manual scans identify accounts without matching active employees, which are investigated and disabled within 10 business days.
