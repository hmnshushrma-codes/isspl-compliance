1. Purpose

This procedure establishes the systematic process for offboarding employees who are leaving Ramdoot Solutions, whether through resignation, termination, retirement, or end of contract. It ensures that all access to organizational systems and data is revoked in a timely manner, company assets are recovered, and knowledge transfer is conducted to minimize operational disruption.

Proper offboarding is critical to protecting information assets and maintaining compliance with access control policies after an employee's departure.

2. Scope

This procedure applies to:

  • All full-time, part-time, and contract employees departing Ramdoot Solutions.
  • Interns and temporary staff at end of engagement.
  • Employees transferring to a different business unit where their current access must be fully revoked.

Vendor/third-party offboarding follows the vendor management lifecycle in RS-PRO-009.

3. Definitions

TermDefinition
Voluntary SeparationEmployee-initiated departure (resignation, retirement).
Involuntary SeparationEmployer-initiated departure (termination for cause, layoff, restructuring).
Last Working Day (LWD)The final day on which the employee is expected to perform duties.
Access RevocationThe process of disabling or removing all system credentials and physical access tokens assigned to the departing employee.
Knowledge TransferStructured handover of work products, documentation, and institutional knowledge to a designated successor or team.

5. Prerequisites

  • Formal resignation letter received (voluntary) or termination decision approved by HR Head and Legal (involuntary).
  • Notice period determined per the employment agreement.
  • Reporting Manager has identified a successor or knowledge transfer recipient.
  • IT Department has an up-to-date inventory of all accounts and assets assigned to the employee.

6. Step-by-Step Procedure

Step 1: Resignation/Termination Trigger

Performed by: HR Department

  1. For voluntary departures: receive and acknowledge the resignation letter; confirm the last working day per the notice period clause.
  2. For involuntary departures: receive the approved termination decision from the authorized approver and Legal counsel.
  3. Create the offboarding ticket in the HRIS with the separation type, effective date, and reason code.
  4. Trigger automated notifications to all stakeholders (Manager, IT, Finance, Facilities, Security).

Step 2: Manager Notification & Planning

Performed by: Reporting Manager

  1. Acknowledge the offboarding notification and review the employee's current assignments, projects, and responsibilities.
  2. Identify a knowledge transfer recipient (successor, team lead, or designated colleague).
  3. Create a knowledge transfer plan with specific deliverables and deadlines before the LWD.
  4. Communicate the departure to the immediate team (following HR guidance on timing and messaging).

Step 3: Access Revocation

Performed by: IT Department

Timing: For involuntary separations, access must be revoked on the same day as the termination notification. For voluntary separations, access is revoked at the end of the last working day.

  1. Disable the corporate email account (set to inactive; do not delete).
  2. Revoke access to all applications, SaaS platforms, cloud consoles, VPN, and remote access systems.
  3. Disable Active Directory / identity provider account.
  4. Remove the user from all security groups, distribution lists, and shared mailboxes.
  5. Revoke code repository access (GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket).
  6. Deactivate or rotate any shared credentials the employee had knowledge of.
  7. Disable physical access (badge, biometric) via the access control system.

Step 4: Email Forwarding & Archival

Performed by: IT Department

  1. Configure an auto-reply on the departing employee's email for 30 days, redirecting contacts to the designated successor.
  2. Set up email forwarding to the Reporting Manager or successor for 90 days (or as directed by HR).
  3. Archive the mailbox contents per the data retention policy.
  4. After the forwarding period, fully disable the mailbox.

Step 5: Asset Return

Performed by: IT Department / Facilities

  1. Collect the following assets on or before the LWD:
    • Laptop/workstation and charger
    • Mobile device (if company-issued)
    • Access badges, keys, and parking permits
    • Any other company-owned hardware (monitors, headsets, peripherals)
  2. Verify all items against the IT Asset Management System inventory.
  3. Securely wipe personal data from returned devices while preserving company data.
  4. Update the asset status to "Returned" in the Asset Management System.
  5. For unreturned assets, escalate to HR and Finance for recovery or payroll deduction.

Step 6: Exit Interview

Performed by: HR Department

  1. Schedule the exit interview at least 3 business days before the LWD.
  2. Conduct the interview covering: reasons for departure, feedback on management, suggestions for improvement, and any unresolved concerns.
  3. Document the exit interview findings and file in the personnel record.
  4. Aggregate anonymized feedback for quarterly HR review.

Step 7: Final Access Audit

Performed by: Information Security Team

  1. Within 24 hours of the LWD, conduct a comprehensive access audit across all systems.
  2. Verify that no active sessions, API keys, service accounts, or tokens remain associated with the departing employee.
  3. Check for any personal devices enrolled in the corporate MDM and remove them.
  4. Confirm that shared/service account passwords known to the employee have been rotated.
  5. Document the audit findings and sign off on the security clearance.

Step 8: Knowledge Transfer

Performed by: Departing Employee / Reporting Manager

  1. Complete the knowledge transfer plan, ensuring all deliverables are handed over.
  2. Transfer ownership of documents, project files, and repositories to the successor.
  3. Conduct handover meetings for ongoing projects and client relationships.
  4. Document any undocumented processes or tribal knowledge.
  5. Reporting Manager signs off on the knowledge transfer completion.

Step 9: Payroll & Benefits Termination

Performed by: Finance/Payroll

  1. Calculate the final settlement including: remaining salary, leave encashment, bonuses, and any outstanding reimbursements.
  2. Deduct any recoverable amounts (advance salary, unreturned assets, notice period shortfall).
  3. Process the final settlement payment within [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] days of the LWD.
  4. Terminate health insurance, provident fund contributions, and other benefits as per policy.
  5. Issue Form 16 / tax-related documents as applicable.

7. Verification / Completion Criteria

Offboarding is considered complete when:

  • All system access has been revoked and verified by the security audit.
  • All company assets have been returned or recovery has been initiated.
  • Exit interview has been conducted and documented.
  • Knowledge transfer has been completed and signed off by the Reporting Manager.
  • Final payroll settlement has been processed.
  • The offboarding checklist is fully signed by HR, IT, Security, Finance, and the Reporting Manager.
  • Employee record in HRIS is updated to "Separated" with the effective date.

9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this procedure require written approval from [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].

  • Immediate Termination (Security Incident): If the departure is triggered by a security incident, access must be revoked immediately upon the decision, bypassing the standard notice period timeline. The Information Security Team leads the offboarding in coordination with Legal.
  • Garden Leave: Employees on garden leave retain their employment status but all system access is revoked on the date garden leave begins.
  • Extended Asset Return: If an employee is in a remote location and cannot return assets by the LWD, a maximum 7-day extension may be granted by the IT Manager with documented courier tracking.
