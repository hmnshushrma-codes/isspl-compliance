1. Purpose

This procedure defines the end-to-end process for onboarding new employees at Ramdoot Solutions. It ensures that every new hire is systematically integrated into the organization with appropriate background verification, legal agreements, IT provisioning, security training, and managerial orientation completed before the employee begins productive work.

The procedure safeguards company information assets by enforcing least-privilege access from day one and ensuring all personnel acknowledge their security responsibilities.

2. Scope

This procedure applies to:

  • All full-time, part-time, and contract employees joining Ramdoot Solutions.
  • Interns and temporary staff engaged for more than 30 days.
  • Employees transferring between departments or business units where a role change triggers re-onboarding activities.

This procedure does not cover vendor or third-party onboarding, which is addressed in RS-PRO-009.

5. Prerequisites

  • A signed offer letter accepted by the candidate.
  • Approved headcount/position in the organizational budget.
  • Completed recruitment workflow with all interview scorecards filed.
  • Hiring Manager has submitted the Access Request Form listing required systems and access levels.
  • IT Department has available hardware stock or procurement lead time is factored into the start date.

6. Step-by-Step Procedure

Step 1: Offer Acceptance

Performed by: HR Department

  1. Issue the formal offer letter via the HRIS platform or email.
  2. Collect the signed offer letter (digital or physical) from the candidate.
  3. Record the accepted start date and notify the Hiring Manager, IT Department, and Finance.
  4. Create the employee record in the HRIS with status "Pre-Joining".

Step 2: Background Check

Performed by: HR Department (via approved background verification agency)

  1. Initiate background verification covering identity, education, previous employment, and criminal records.
  2. Track verification progress; escalate delays to the HR Manager.
  3. Upon satisfactory completion, update the HRIS record and file the verification report.
  4. If adverse findings are discovered, escalate to HR Manager and Legal for a decision before the start date.

Step 3: NDA & Agreements

Performed by: HR Department / Legal

  1. Present the following documents for signature on or before Day 1:
    • Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
    • Acceptable Use Policy acknowledgement
    • Employee Code of Conduct
    • Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement
    • Data Protection and Privacy acknowledgement
  2. Verify all documents are signed and filed in the employee's personnel folder.
  3. Provide copies to the employee for their records.

Step 4: IT Asset Provisioning

Performed by: IT Department

  1. Prepare the assigned laptop/workstation with the standard operating system image.
  2. Install required software per the role-specific software matrix.
  3. Enroll the device in the endpoint management and antivirus platform.
  4. Enable full-disk encryption and verify the device meets the hardware security baseline.
  5. Record the asset serial number and assign it to the employee in the IT Asset Management System.
  6. Prepare any additional peripherals (monitor, headset, mobile device) as required.

Step 5: Account Creation (Least Privilege)

Performed by: IT Department

  1. Create the employee's corporate email account and directory profile.
  2. Provision access to core platforms (email, intranet, HRIS self-service) as baseline.
  3. Review the Hiring Manager's Access Request Form and provision only the systems and roles explicitly listed.
  4. Apply the principle of least privilege — no administrative or elevated access unless separately approved through the Privileged Access Procedure (RS-PRO-004).
  5. Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all provisioned accounts.
  6. Document all access granted in the Access Management Log.

Step 6: Security Awareness Training

Performed by: Information Security Team

  1. Schedule the mandatory Security Awareness Training session within the first 5 business days.
  2. Training must cover: information security policies, acceptable use, password management, phishing awareness, social engineering, incident reporting, and data classification.
  3. Administer the post-training assessment; a minimum score of 80% is required to pass.
  4. Record the training completion and assessment score in the Training Management System.
  5. If the employee fails the assessment, schedule a re-take within 5 business days.

Step 7: System Access Setup

Performed by: IT Department / Hiring Manager

  1. Walk the new employee through their provisioned systems and verify they can log in.
  2. Confirm VPN connectivity and remote access functionality if the role is remote-eligible.
  3. Verify access to shared drives, project repositories, and collaboration tools.
  4. Ensure the employee understands how to request additional access via the Access Request Procedure (RS-PRO-003).

Step 8: Manager Orientation

Performed by: Hiring Manager

  1. Conduct a one-on-one orientation covering:
    • Team structure and key contacts
    • Project overview and immediate deliverables
    • Communication norms and meeting cadence
    • Performance expectations and KPIs
  2. Introduce the employee to team members and the assigned buddy/mentor.
  3. Provide access to team-specific documentation and knowledge bases.
  4. Schedule recurring 1:1 check-ins for the probation period.

Step 9: Probation Review Schedule

Performed by: Hiring Manager / HR Department

  1. Set the probation period duration as per the employment agreement (typically 3–6 months).
  2. Schedule formal review checkpoints at 30, 60, and 90 days (and at 150 and 180 days if a 6-month probation).
  3. Document each review in the HRIS with outcomes and any corrective actions.
  4. At the end of the probation period, issue a confirmation of employment or extend probation with documented justification.

7. Verification / Completion Criteria

The onboarding process is considered complete when all of the following are verified:

  • Background check report filed with satisfactory results.
  • All agreements (NDA, AUP, Code of Conduct, IP Assignment, Privacy) are signed and filed.
  • IT assets provisioned, enrolled in endpoint management, and recorded in the Asset Management System.
  • All accounts created with least-privilege access and MFA enabled.
  • Security Awareness Training completed with a passing score.
  • Manager orientation completed and buddy/mentor assigned.
  • Probation review schedule established in the HRIS.
  • Onboarding checklist signed off by HR, IT, and the Hiring Manager.

9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this procedure must be approved in writing by [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] and documented with a justification.

  • Emergency Hires: If business urgency requires an employee to start before background check completion, the Hiring Manager and HR Head must jointly approve a conditional start. The employee's access is limited to non-sensitive systems until the background check is completed.
  • Contractor Conversion: Contractors converting to full-time employment may have abbreviated onboarding if their background check is current (within 12 months) and existing agreements cover the new role's requirements.
  • Re-hires: Former employees re-joining within 12 months may skip background checks if the previous report is on file, subject to HR approval.
