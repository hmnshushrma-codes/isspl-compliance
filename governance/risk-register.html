1. Purpose

The Risk Register is a living document that captures all identified information security, operational, and compliance risks faced by Ramdoot Solutions. It provides a structured approach to risk identification, assessment, treatment, and monitoring, supporting the organisation's overall risk management programme.

This register is maintained by the CISO/Security Officer and reviewed quarterly by the Security and Compliance Committee. It aligns with the risk management requirements of SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC3.1 - CC3.4) and the organisation's Compliance Policy (RS-GOV-002).

2. How to Use This Register

Instructions for Risk Owners:

  1. Identify: When a new risk is identified (through assessments, incidents, audits, or day-to-day operations), add a new row with a unique Risk ID.
  2. Assess: Rate the Likelihood (1-5) and Impact (1-5) to calculate the Risk Score (Likelihood x Impact).
  3. Treat: Select a treatment strategy (Mitigate, Accept, Transfer, or Avoid) and document the specific controls that address the risk.
  4. Assign: Designate a Risk Owner who is accountable for managing and monitoring the risk.
  5. Monitor: Review each risk at least quarterly. Update the status, likelihood, impact, and controls as the risk landscape changes.
  6. Report: Present risk register updates to the Security and Compliance Committee quarterly and to the Board annually.

3. Risk Scoring Methodology

3.1 Likelihood Scale

Score Rating Description Frequency
1 Rare May only occur in exceptional circumstances Less than once in 5 years
2 Unlikely Could occur at some time but not expected Once in 2-5 years
3 Possible Might occur at some time Once in 1-2 years
4 Likely Will probably occur in most circumstances Several times per year
5 Almost Certain Expected to occur in most circumstances Monthly or more frequently

3.2 Impact Scale

Score Rating Financial Impact Operational Impact Reputational Impact
1 Insignificant Negligible financial loss No disruption to operations No external awareness
2 Minor Small financial loss, absorbed within budget Minor disruption, resolved within hours Local/limited awareness
3 Moderate Significant financial loss requiring management attention Partial disruption, resolved within 1-2 days Industry awareness, media interest
4 Major Major financial loss impacting profitability Major disruption, several days to recover National media, client attrition
5 Catastrophic Existential financial loss, threat to business survival Complete operational failure, weeks to recover Widespread media, regulatory action, loss of major clients

3.3 Risk Score Matrix

Likelihood \ Impact 1 (Insignificant) 2 (Minor) 3 (Moderate) 4 (Major) 5 (Catastrophic)
5 (Almost Certain) 5 10 15 20 25
4 (Likely) 4 8 12 16 20
3 (Possible) 3 6 9 12 15
2 (Unlikely) 2 4 6 8 10
1 (Rare) 1 2 3 4 5

Risk Appetite:

  • Low (1-4): Acceptable. Monitor during regular reviews.
  • Medium (5-9): Tolerable with controls. Active monitoring required.
  • High (10-15): Unacceptable. Immediate mitigation or escalation required.
  • Critical (16-25): Intolerable. Requires immediate executive attention and action.

5. Risk Treatment Strategies

Strategy Description When to Use
Mitigate Implement controls to reduce the likelihood and/or impact of the risk to an acceptable level When the risk can be reduced through feasible and cost-effective controls
Accept Acknowledge the risk and accept the potential consequences without additional controls When the risk is within appetite, or the cost of mitigation exceeds the potential impact
Transfer Transfer the risk to a third party (e.g., through insurance or outsourcing) When the risk is best managed by a specialised third party or can be covered by insurance
Avoid Eliminate the risk by ceasing the activity that generates it When the risk is intolerable and no effective mitigation is available
