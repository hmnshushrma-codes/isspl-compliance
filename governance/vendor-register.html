1. Purpose

The Vendor Register is a centralised inventory of all third-party vendors, service providers, and suppliers engaged by Ramdoot Solutions. It supports the Vendor Management Policy by providing visibility into vendor relationships, their criticality to operations, data access levels, contractual status, and compliance posture.

Effective vendor management is a key requirement under SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC9.2) and is essential for managing supply chain risk, ensuring data protection, and maintaining compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

2. How to Use This Register

Instructions for Vendor Owners:

  1. New Vendor: Before engaging any new vendor, add an entry to this register. Assign a unique Vendor ID (format: VND-XXX).
  2. Criticality Assessment: Classify the vendor as Critical, Standard, or Low based on service dependency and data access.
  3. Data Access: Document whether the vendor has access to Ramdoot Solutions' data, client data, or personal data.
  4. Risk Assessment: Complete a vendor risk assessment using the Vendor Security Questionnaire before onboarding Critical and Standard vendors.
  5. Contract Review: Ensure contracts include appropriate security, confidentiality, data protection, and audit right clauses.
  6. Ongoing Monitoring: Review Critical vendors quarterly and all other vendors at least annually. Collect updated SOC reports or ISO certificates.
  7. Offboarding: When a vendor relationship ends, ensure access is revoked, data is returned/destroyed, and the register is updated.

3. Criticality Definitions

Level Definition Assessment Frequency Examples
Critical Vendor failure would cause significant disruption to core business operations or expose sensitive data. Vendor has direct access to production systems or personal/confidential data. Quarterly review, annual full reassessment Cloud hosting provider, payment processor, core SaaS platform
Standard Vendor provides important services but short-term disruption is manageable. May have limited data access. Annual review Email service, project management tools, HR platform
Low Vendor provides non-critical services with no data access. Disruption has minimal business impact. Annual review Office supplies, catering, general consultants

5. Vendor Assessment Checklist

The following checklist should be completed for all Critical and Standard vendors during onboarding and at each reassessment cycle:

Assessment Area Check Items Evidence Required
Security Certifications Does the vendor hold SOC 2, ISO 27001, or equivalent certifications? Current SOC 2 report, ISO certificate, or bridge letter
Data Protection Does the vendor have adequate data protection controls? Is there a DPA in place? Data Processing Agreement, privacy policy, encryption documentation
Access Controls Does the vendor implement MFA, RBAC, and audit logging? Security questionnaire response, access control documentation
Incident Response Does the vendor have a documented incident response plan with notification timelines? Incident response policy, notification SLAs in contract
Business Continuity Does the vendor have BCP/DR capabilities? What are their RTO/RPO commitments? BCP documentation, SLA, uptime commitments
Sub-processors Does the vendor use sub-processors? Are they disclosed and assessed? Sub-processor list, contractual flow-down provisions
Data Location Where is data stored and processed? Any cross-border transfers? Data residency documentation, transfer mechanisms (if applicable)
Contract Terms Do contracts include security obligations, audit rights, and termination provisions? Contract review checklist, signed agreement
