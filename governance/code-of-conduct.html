1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical standards, professional behaviour, and compliance expectations for all employees, contractors, and stakeholders of Ramdoot Solutions. It defines the principles by which Ramdoot Solutions operates and the responsibilities each individual bears in upholding those principles.

Every person associated with Ramdoot Solutions is expected to read, understand, and abide by this Code. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contractual relationship, and referral to appropriate legal authorities where warranted.

2. Scope

This Code applies to:

  • All full-time and part-time employees of Ramdoot Solutions
  • Temporary staff, interns, and apprentices
  • Independent contractors, consultants, and freelancers engaged by Ramdoot Solutions
  • Members of the Board of Directors and senior management
  • Third-party representatives acting on behalf of Ramdoot Solutions

This Code applies to conduct in all work-related settings, including offices, client sites, company-sponsored events, online communications, and any situation where an individual is representing Ramdoot Solutions.

3. Ethical Behaviour and Integrity

Ramdoot Solutions is committed to conducting business with the highest standards of honesty and integrity. All personnel must:

  • Act honestly and in good faith in all business dealings
  • Provide accurate, complete, and timely information in all reports, records, and communications
  • Never falsify or manipulate data, records, financial statements, or any company documentation
  • Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards in India and any jurisdiction where Ramdoot Solutions operates
  • Treat colleagues, clients, vendors, and the public with respect, fairness, and professionalism
  • Take responsibility for their actions and decisions
  • Avoid any conduct that could damage the reputation or credibility of Ramdoot Solutions

3.1 Compliance with Laws and Regulations

All personnel must comply with applicable Indian laws, including but not limited to:

  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 (as amended) and IT Rules
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)
  • The Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • The Companies Act, 2013
  • The Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988
  • Applicable labour laws and employment regulations
  • Tax and financial reporting regulations

4. Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest arises when a personal interest or relationship interferes, or appears to interfere, with an individual's ability to act in the best interests of Ramdoot Solutions. All personnel must:

  • Avoid situations where personal interests conflict with, or appear to conflict with, the interests of Ramdoot Solutions
  • Disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest immediately to their reporting manager and the Compliance Officer
  • Not engage in any outside employment, consultancy, or business activity that competes with or conflicts with their duties at Ramdoot Solutions without prior written approval
  • Not use company resources, information, or their position for personal gain
  • Recuse themselves from decision-making processes where a conflict exists

4.1 Examples of Conflicts of Interest

  • Having a financial interest in a vendor, supplier, or competitor of Ramdoot Solutions
  • Employing or supervising a family member or close personal associate without proper disclosure
  • Accepting gifts, hospitality, or favours from clients or vendors beyond de minimis value
  • Engaging in outside business activities that use company time, resources, or confidential information
  • Making business decisions influenced by personal relationships rather than merit

  • Infrequent and not part of a pattern
  • Not intended to influence business decisions
  • Consistent with customary business practices
  • Properly documented and reported to the Compliance Officer if above a threshold value

5. Confidentiality and Information Security

Ramdoot Solutions handles sensitive information belonging to the company, its clients, employees, and partners. All personnel must:

  • Protect confidential information from unauthorised access, disclosure, modification, or destruction
  • Not disclose proprietary, trade-secret, or otherwise confidential information to any unauthorised person, whether inside or outside the organisation
  • Use confidential information solely for legitimate business purposes
  • Comply with all information security policies, including the Information Security Policy (RS-SEC-001) and Data Classification Policy (RS-SEC-006)
  • Safeguard login credentials, access tokens, and other authentication mechanisms
  • Report any suspected data breach, information leak, or security incident immediately in accordance with the Incident Response Plan (RS-SEC-004)
  • Return all confidential information and company property upon termination of employment or contractual engagement

5.1 Intellectual Property

All intellectual property created during the course of employment or engagement with Ramdoot Solutions is the property of Ramdoot Solutions unless otherwise agreed in writing. Personnel must:

  • Respect the intellectual property rights of Ramdoot Solutions, its clients, and third parties
  • Not use unlicensed software, copyrighted materials, or patented technologies without authorisation
  • Protect source code, algorithms, designs, and other proprietary materials

6. Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination

Ramdoot Solutions is committed to maintaining a work environment free from all forms of harassment, discrimination, and bullying. This commitment extends to all interactions in the workplace, at company events, and in any context where individuals represent Ramdoot Solutions.

6.1 Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is strictly prohibited:

  • Sexual Harassment: Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favours, or other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature, as defined under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (PoSH Act)
  • Verbal Harassment: Offensive comments, slurs, jokes, or remarks relating to a person's gender, caste, religion, race, ethnicity, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic
  • Physical Harassment: Unwelcome physical contact, intimidation, threats, or assault
  • Workplace Bullying: Repeated unreasonable behaviour directed towards an employee or group of employees that creates a risk to health and safety
  • Discrimination: Treating individuals unfairly or unequally based on any protected characteristic in hiring, promotion, compensation, or any other employment decision
  • Cyber Harassment: Harassment through electronic communications, including email, messaging platforms, social media, or any digital medium

7. Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

Ramdoot Solutions maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards bribery and corruption in all forms. This applies to dealings with government officials, private sector entities, clients, vendors, and any third party.

7.1 Prohibited Activities

  • Offering, giving, soliciting, or accepting any bribe, kickback, or improper payment to or from any person
  • Making facilitation payments to expedite routine government actions
  • Providing or accepting gifts, entertainment, or hospitality that could be perceived as an attempt to influence business decisions
  • Using intermediaries, agents, or consultants to circumvent anti-bribery prohibitions
  • Making charitable donations or sponsorships as a disguise for improper payments

7.2 Compliance Requirements

  • All payments, expenses, and financial transactions must be accurately recorded in the company's books and records
  • Personnel must conduct due diligence on third parties before engaging in business relationships
  • Any request, offer, or suspicion of bribery must be reported immediately to the Compliance Officer
  • Personnel must comply with the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, and any other applicable anti-corruption legislation

8. Use of Company Resources

Company resources including equipment, software, facilities, funds, and data are provided for legitimate business purposes. Personnel must:

  • Use company resources responsibly and primarily for business purposes
  • Not misuse, damage, or waste company resources
  • Follow the Acceptable Use Policy (RS-SEC-005) when using company IT systems and networks
  • Not install unauthorised software or connect personal devices to company networks without approval
  • Protect company property from theft, loss, or damage
  • Report any loss, damage, or misuse of company resources promptly

10. Whistleblower Protection

Ramdoot Solutions is firmly committed to protecting individuals who report violations in good faith. Retaliation against whistleblowers is strictly prohibited.

10.1 Protections

  • No Retaliation: No employee will be subjected to retaliation, including termination, demotion, suspension, threats, harassment, or discrimination, for making a good-faith report of a violation
  • Confidentiality: The identity of the reporter will be kept confidential to the maximum extent possible, consistent with the need to conduct an adequate investigation and comply with applicable laws
  • Anonymous Reporting: Reports may be submitted anonymously through the whistleblower hotline. Anonymous reports will be investigated to the extent feasible
  • Legal Protection: Whistleblowers are protected under applicable Indian law, including provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 (Section 177) and relevant judicial precedents

10.2 What Does Not Constitute Good Faith

  • Filing deliberately false or malicious reports
  • Using the reporting mechanism to pursue personal grievances unrelated to genuine compliance concerns
  • Making reports with the intent to harm or discredit another person without reasonable basis

Deliberate misuse of the reporting system may itself be subject to disciplinary action.

11. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be addressed through appropriate disciplinary measures, which may include:

  • Verbal or written warning
  • Mandatory training or counselling
  • Suspension with or without pay
  • Demotion or reassignment
  • Termination of employment or contractual engagement
  • Recovery of damages or losses caused by the violation
  • Referral to law enforcement or regulatory authorities where appropriate

The severity of disciplinary action will depend on the nature, gravity, and frequency of the violation, and whether the individual cooperated with the investigation.
