1. Purpose

This document defines the roles and responsibilities for security, compliance, and governance activities at Ramdoot Solutions using the RACI (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed) framework. Clear assignment of responsibilities ensures that all compliance and security activities are owned, executed, reviewed, and reported effectively.

2. Scope

This matrix covers all security and compliance activities across the organisation, including policy management, risk assessment, access control, incident response, vendor management, training, audit, and business continuity planning. It applies to all roles within Ramdoot Solutions that have a part in the compliance programme.

3. RACI Legend

Code Role Definition
R Responsible Performs the work. The person or role that executes the activity. Multiple roles may be Responsible.
A Accountable Ultimately answerable. The single person or role with decision-making authority and final accountability. Only one 'A' per activity.
C Consulted Provides input before the activity is performed. Two-way communication.
I Informed Notified after the activity is performed or a decision is made. One-way communication.

6. Governance Structure

The following governance structure supports the roles and responsibilities defined in this matrix:

6.1 Board of Directors

  • Sets the strategic direction for security and compliance
  • Approves high-level policies and risk appetite
  • Receives quarterly compliance reports and annual programme reviews
  • Ensures adequate resources are allocated to the compliance programme

6.2 Security and Compliance Committee

A cross-functional committee that meets [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] to:

  • Review the risk register and treatment plans
  • Assess compliance programme effectiveness
  • Discuss security incidents and lessons learned
  • Coordinate audit preparation and remediation efforts
  • Review and approve policy changes

7. Review and Maintenance

This matrix shall be reviewed:

  • At least annually as part of the compliance programme review cycle
  • Whenever there is a significant organisational change (e.g., new hires in key roles, restructuring)
  • After any audit finding related to unclear ownership or accountability gaps
  • When new compliance requirements are introduced that alter role expectations
