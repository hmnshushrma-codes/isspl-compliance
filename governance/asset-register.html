1. Purpose

The IT Asset Register provides a comprehensive inventory of all information technology assets owned, leased, or managed by Ramdoot Solutions. Maintaining an accurate asset inventory is fundamental to information security, enabling the organisation to protect what it owns, track asset lifecycle, manage configurations, and ensure compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and internal security policies.

This register supports requirements under SOC 2 CC6.1 (logical and physical access controls) and CC6.4 (restrictions on physical access to assets), as well as the organisation's Data Classification Policy (RS-SEC-006) and Information Security Policy (RS-SEC-001).

2. How to Use This Register

Instructions for Asset Owners:

  1. Registration: Every IT asset must be registered before deployment. Assign a unique Asset ID following the naming convention (see Section 3).
  2. Classification: Classify each asset according to the data classification scheme (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) based on the highest sensitivity of data it stores, processes, or transmits.
  3. Ownership: Assign a named owner responsible for the asset's security, maintenance, and eventual disposal.
  4. Lifecycle Tracking: Record acquisition date and expected end-of-life. Plan replacements before end-of-life to avoid unsupported hardware or software.
  5. Status Updates: Update the status whenever an asset is deployed, redeployed, decommissioned, lost, or stolen.
  6. Quarterly Reconciliation: The IT Manager reconciles the register against physical inventory and system discovery tools quarterly.
  7. Disposal: Decommissioned assets must follow the secure disposal procedure, including data wiping and documentation.

5. Data Classification Levels

Level Description Examples Handling Requirements
Restricted Most sensitive data; unauthorised disclosure would cause severe harm Database credentials, encryption keys, client financial data Encrypted at rest and in transit, strict access control, audit logging
Confidential Sensitive business data; disclosure could cause significant harm Source code, client data, employee PII, contracts Encrypted, access limited to authorised personnel, NDA required for external parties
Internal Internal business data not intended for external distribution Policies, internal communications, project plans Access limited to employees and authorised contractors
Public Data approved for public distribution Marketing materials, published documentation, public website content No restrictions on distribution
