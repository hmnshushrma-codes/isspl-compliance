1. Purpose

This Compliance Policy establishes the framework for Ramdoot Solutions' compliance programme. It defines the structures, roles, and processes that ensure the organisation meets its regulatory obligations, maintains industry certifications, and operates in accordance with applicable laws and internal policies.

The compliance programme supports Ramdoot Solutions' commitment to ethical business practices, data protection, information security, and the trust placed in the organisation by its clients, employees, and stakeholders.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who operate within Ramdoot Solutions' systems, processes, or facilities. It encompasses:

  • Regulatory compliance (Indian and international law)
  • Industry framework alignment (SOC 2 Type II, SOC 1 where applicable)
  • Internal policy governance and enforcement
  • Audit readiness and support
  • Continuous monitoring and improvement

3. Compliance Programme Overview

Ramdoot Solutions' compliance programme is structured around the following pillars:

Compliance Programme Pillars:

  1. Governance: Clear ownership, roles, and accountability for compliance activities
  2. Policies and Procedures: Comprehensive, regularly reviewed documentation
  3. Risk Management: Proactive identification, assessment, and mitigation of compliance risks
  4. Training and Awareness: Ongoing education to ensure all personnel understand their obligations
  5. Monitoring and Auditing: Continuous and periodic assessment of compliance effectiveness
  6. Incident Management: Defined processes for identifying, reporting, and resolving non-compliance
  7. Continuous Improvement: Lessons learned and corrective actions driving programme maturity

6. SOC 2 and SOC 1 Alignment

6.1 SOC 2 Alignment

Ramdoot Solutions aligns its controls and processes with the AICPA Trust Services Criteria for SOC 2 reporting. The compliance programme supports the following Trust Services Categories:

  • Security (Common Criteria): Protection of information and systems against unauthorised access, both logical and physical
  • Availability: Systems and information are available for operation and use as committed
  • Processing Integrity: System processing is complete, valid, accurate, timely, and authorised
  • Confidentiality: Information designated as confidential is protected as committed
  • Privacy: Personal information is collected, used, retained, disclosed, and disposed of in conformity with commitments

The SOC 2 readiness documentation is maintained in the SOC 2 Documentation section.

6.2 SOC 1 Alignment

Where Ramdoot Solutions provides services that impact clients' internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR), the organisation maintains SOC 1 readiness documentation. SOC 1 applicability is assessed on a client-by-client basis.

The SOC 1 readiness documentation is maintained in the SOC 1 Documentation section.

7. Policy Management

Ramdoot Solutions maintains a comprehensive suite of compliance documentation, tracked through the Master Policy Register (RS-REG-008). Policy management follows these principles:

7.1 Policy Lifecycle

  1. Drafting: Policies are drafted by the responsible owner in collaboration with subject matter experts
  2. Review: Draft policies undergo review by relevant stakeholders, including legal, HR, IT, and the Compliance Officer
  3. Approval: Policies are approved by the designated approver (typically senior management or the Board for high-level policies)
  4. Communication: Approved policies are distributed to all affected personnel and acknowledged
  5. Implementation: Supporting procedures, controls, and training are established
  6. Monitoring: Compliance with policies is monitored through audits, reviews, and incident analysis
  7. Review: All policies are reviewed at least annually, or upon significant changes to the business, technology, or regulatory environment
  8. Retirement: Obsolete policies are retired, archived, and removed from active distribution

  • Internal audit scope covers policy adherence, control effectiveness, and gap identification
  • Findings are documented, assigned corrective actions, and tracked to resolution
  • Internal audit reports are presented to senior management and the Board

8.2 External Audits

  • Ramdoot Solutions cooperates fully with external auditors engaged for SOC 2, SOC 1, or other certification audits
  • The Compliance Officer serves as the primary liaison for external auditors
  • All personnel must provide accurate and complete information to auditors
  • Evidence artefacts are maintained in the Evidence Library
  • Audit findings and management responses are tracked in the audit management process

8.3 Regulatory Inspections

In the event of a regulatory inspection or inquiry, the Compliance Officer must be notified immediately. All communications with regulatory bodies are to be coordinated through the Compliance Officer and legal counsel.

9. Non-Compliance Consequences

Non-compliance with this policy, related policies, or applicable laws and regulations may have serious consequences for individuals and the organisation.

9.1 Individual Consequences

  • Verbal or written warning
  • Mandatory retraining
  • Performance impact and review
  • Suspension with or without pay
  • Termination of employment or contractual engagement
  • Personal civil or criminal liability under applicable law

9.2 Organisational Consequences

  • Regulatory fines and penalties
  • Loss of certifications or audit qualifications
  • Contractual breach with clients, resulting in liability or loss of business
  • Reputational damage
  • Legal proceedings from affected parties

9.3 Escalation Matrix

Severity Examples Escalation To Response Timeline

Medium Repeated minor violations, access control lapse Department Head, Compliance Officer, HR Within 2 business days
High Data breach, regulatory violation, fraud suspicion Senior Management, Legal, Compliance Officer Within 24 hours
