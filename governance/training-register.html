1. Purpose

The Security Training Register tracks all security awareness training, phishing simulations, role-based security training, and compliance training completed by Ramdoot Solutions personnel. Maintaining accurate training records ensures the organisation can demonstrate compliance with training requirements under SOC 2, internal policies, and applicable regulations.

This register supports SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria CC1.4 (competence and accountability), CC2.2 (communication of internal control information), and the organisation's Security Awareness Training Programme as defined in the Information Security Policy (RS-SEC-001).

2. How to Use This Register

Instructions for HR / Training Coordinators:

  1. New Hire: Add a new row for each employee upon joining. Schedule Security Awareness training within the first [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] days of employment.
  2. Training Completion: After each training event, update the register with the training date, completion status, and any score or result.
  3. Phishing Simulations: Record phishing simulation results for each participant, including whether they clicked, reported, or ignored the simulated email.
  4. Role-Based Training: Assign role-based training based on the employee's function (developers, IT admins, managers). Track completion separately.
  5. Recurrence: All mandatory training must be repeated annually. Set the Next Due Date for 12 months from completion.
  6. Non-Completion: Employees who fail to complete mandatory training within the due date are flagged. Escalate to their manager and the Compliance Officer.
  7. Reporting: Generate monthly completion reports and quarterly training programme effectiveness reports for the Security and Compliance Committee.

Target Metrics:

  • Phishing click rate: Target below 5% (industry benchmark: 10-15%)
  • Report rate: Target above 60% (employees reporting suspicious emails to IT/Security)

7. Metrics and Reporting

The following metrics are tracked and reported quarterly:

  • Training completion rate: Percentage of employees who completed mandatory training by the due date (target: 100%)
  • Average assessment score: Mean score across Security Awareness quiz (target: >= 85%)
  • Phishing click rate: Percentage of employees who clicked simulated phishing links (target: < 5%)
  • Phishing report rate: Percentage of employees who reported simulated phishing emails (target: > 60%)
  • Overdue training: Count of employees with overdue mandatory training
  • New hire training SLA: Percentage of new hires completing Security Awareness within the onboarding SLA
  • Remedial training: Count of employees receiving additional training after phishing clicks or policy violations
