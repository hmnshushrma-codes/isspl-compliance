2. Purpose

This Data Retention Policy defines the requirements for retaining, archiving, and securely disposing of data and records maintained by Ramdoot Solutions. The policy ensures that data is retained for the minimum period necessary to meet legal, regulatory, contractual, and business requirements, and that data beyond its retention period is disposed of securely to reduce risk exposure.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All data and records created, received, stored, or processed by Ramdoot Solutions, in any format (electronic, paper, audio, video).
  • All information systems, databases, archives, backups, cloud storage, email systems, and physical file repositories.
  • All employees, contractors, and third parties who create, manage, or dispose of Ramdoot Solutions data.

4. Objectives

  1. Define minimum and maximum retention periods for different categories of data.
  2. Ensure compliance with Indian legal and regulatory retention requirements.
  3. Establish procedures for legal holds to preserve data relevant to ongoing or anticipated litigation.
  4. Define secure disposal methods aligned with NIST SP 800-88 guidelines.
  5. Minimize risk by ensuring data is not retained longer than necessary.
  6. Establish archival requirements for data that must be retained long-term.

6. Roles and Responsibilities

6.1 Information Security Officer

The ISO shall maintain this policy, define retention schedules in coordination with legal and business stakeholders, and oversee compliance with retention and disposal requirements.

6.2 Legal / Compliance

Legal counsel shall advise on retention periods mandated by Indian law, initiate and manage legal holds, and approve the release of legal holds.

6.3 Data Owners

Data owners shall ensure that data under their responsibility is retained in accordance with the retention schedule and shall initiate disposal when retention periods expire (subject to any active legal holds).

6.4 IT Department / Data Custodians

IT shall implement technical controls for data retention, archival, backup, and secure disposal. IT shall maintain disposal logs and certificates of destruction.

6.5 All Employees

Employees shall comply with retention schedules, shall not delete data subject to legal holds, and shall not retain data beyond its approved retention period without authorization.

7. Policy Statements

7.1 Retention Principles

Data shall be retained only for as long as required to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, satisfy legal and regulatory requirements, meet contractual obligations, and support legitimate business needs. Data shall not be retained indefinitely without justification.

7.3 Legal Holds

When Ramdoot Solutions becomes aware of pending or reasonably anticipated litigation, regulatory investigation, or audit, a legal hold shall be issued. Legal holds override normal retention schedules — data subject to a legal hold must not be modified, deleted, or destroyed until the hold is released by legal counsel. Legal holds shall be communicated in writing to all relevant data custodians. Compliance with legal holds shall be documented and monitored.

7.4 Secure Disposal

When data reaches the end of its retention period and is not subject to a legal hold, it shall be securely disposed of using methods aligned with NIST SP 800-88:

  • Clear: Overwriting data using standard write commands — suitable for Internal data on media that will be reused within the organization.
  • Purge: Using techniques that render data recovery infeasible (e.g., cryptographic erase, degaussing) — suitable for Confidential data.
  • Destroy: Physical destruction of media (shredding, disintegration, incineration) — required for Restricted data and for media that will leave organizational control.

7.5 Archival

Data that must be retained beyond its active use period shall be moved to approved archival storage. Archived data shall remain accessible for retrieval within [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] business days if required. Archives shall be encrypted and access-controlled consistent with the data's classification level. Archived data shall be periodically tested for integrity and readability.

7.6 Backup Retention

Backup copies shall be retained in accordance with the retention period of the source data. When source data is disposed of, corresponding backup copies shall also be identified and disposed of within a reasonable timeframe. Backup media shall be tracked and included in disposal schedules.

8. Detailed Requirements

8.1 Indian Legal Requirements Reference

Ramdoot Solutions shall comply with all applicable Indian data retention laws, including but not limited to:

  • Companies Act, 2013: Financial books and records to be maintained for 8 financial years preceding the current year (Section 128).
  • Income Tax Act, 1961: Books of account and related documents to be retained for a minimum of 6 years from the end of the relevant assessment year (Section 44AA, Rule 6F); longer in cases of pending proceedings.
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: Personal data shall not be retained beyond the period necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Data fiduciaries must erase personal data when consent is withdrawn or the specified purpose is fulfilled.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000: Bodies corporate handling sensitive personal data must implement reasonable security practices and maintain records as prescribed.
  • Goods and Services Tax Act: GST-related records to be retained for 6 years from the due date of filing the annual return.

The ISO and legal counsel shall maintain a register of applicable legal retention requirements and update the retention schedule accordingly.

8.2 Disposal Verification

All disposal actions shall be documented in a disposal log including: data description, classification level, disposal method, date, and personnel responsible. For Confidential and Restricted data, a certificate of destruction shall be obtained (from an internal authorized person or a contracted disposal vendor). Disposal logs and certificates shall themselves be retained for 7 years.

8.3 Third-Party Data

When Ramdoot Solutions holds data on behalf of clients or partners, retention and disposal shall comply with the terms of the applicable contract or data processing agreement. Upon termination of a client relationship, client data shall be returned or securely disposed of as specified in the contract, within [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] days.

8.4 Cloud Data Retention

Data stored in cloud services shall be subject to the same retention and disposal requirements as on-premises data. Cloud service provider data deletion capabilities and certifications shall be verified. Where cloud providers cannot guarantee secure deletion, compensating controls (e.g., encryption with key destruction) shall be used.

9. Exceptions

Exceptions to retention periods or disposal methods require written justification, a risk assessment, and approval from the ISO and legal counsel. Exceptions shall be documented, time-bound (maximum 12 months), and reviewed at expiry. Legal holds are not exceptions — they are mandatory overrides.

10. Monitoring and Compliance

  • Annual review of the retention schedule to ensure alignment with current legal and business requirements.
  • Quarterly review of disposal logs to verify timely and compliant disposal of expired data.
  • Monitoring of legal hold compliance — ensuring no data under hold is modified or destroyed.
  • Internal audits shall include verification of retention and disposal practices.
  • Automated tools shall be deployed where feasible to flag data that has exceeded its retention period.

11. Violations and Enforcement

Unauthorized destruction of data (particularly data under legal hold), failure to dispose of data beyond its retention period, and retention of data without authorization constitute policy violations. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Destruction of data under legal hold may expose the organization and the individual to legal penalties — such violations shall be escalated to legal counsel immediately.

12. Records and Evidence

  • Approved retention schedule (current and historical versions).
  • Disposal logs with dates, methods, and responsible personnel.
  • Certificates of destruction.
  • Legal hold notices and release records.
  • Archive access and retrieval logs.
  • Exception request forms and approvals.
  • Annual retention schedule review records.

15. Review Cycle

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually, or sooner upon:

  • Changes in Indian data protection, tax, or corporate law that affect retention requirements.
  • New contractual obligations that introduce specific retention requirements.
  • Legal hold events or litigation outcomes that highlight process gaps.
  • Audit findings related to data retention or disposal.
