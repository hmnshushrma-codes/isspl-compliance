2. Purpose

This Access Control Policy defines the principles, requirements, and procedures governing the management of logical and physical access to Ramdoot Solutions information systems, networks, applications, and data. The policy ensures that access is granted on a need-to-know basis and in alignment with the principle of least privilege, protecting information assets from unauthorized access, modification, or destruction.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and third-party users who require access to Ramdoot Solutions information systems.
  • All information systems, applications, databases, cloud services, network infrastructure, and physical facilities owned or operated by Ramdoot Solutions.
  • All types of access, including local, remote, VPN, API, and administrative access.

4. Objectives

  1. Ensure that access to information assets is authorized, controlled, and auditable.
  2. Implement role-based access control (RBAC) to align access rights with job functions.
  3. Enforce the principle of least privilege — users shall receive only the minimum access required to perform their duties.
  4. Establish processes for timely provisioning and deprovisioning of access.
  5. Define requirements for periodic access reviews to detect and remediate inappropriate access.
  6. Manage privileged access with enhanced controls and monitoring.

5. Definitions

6. Roles and Responsibilities

6.1 Information Security Officer

The ISO shall define access control standards, oversee quarterly access reviews, and approve exceptions to this policy.

6.2 IT Administration

IT shall implement access control mechanisms, process provisioning and deprovisioning requests, maintain the identity management system, and generate access reports for review.

6.3 Department Managers / Data Owners

Managers shall authorize access requests for their team members, participate in quarterly access reviews, and ensure that departing employees' access is revoked promptly.

6.4 Human Resources

HR shall notify IT of all personnel changes — new hires, role changes, transfers, and terminations — in a timely manner to trigger access provisioning or deprovisioning actions.

6.5 All Users

Users shall safeguard their credentials, shall not share access with others, and shall report suspected unauthorized access immediately.

7. Policy Statements

7.1 Principle of Least Privilege

All access to Ramdoot Solutions information systems shall be granted based on the principle of least privilege. Users shall receive only the access necessary to perform their assigned job functions. No user shall be granted blanket or unrestricted access to any system.

7.2 Role-Based Access Control

Ramdoot Solutions shall implement RBAC as the primary access control model. Roles shall be defined based on job functions and shall be reviewed annually. Each role shall have a documented set of permissions. Users shall be assigned to roles — not granted individual permissions — except where documented exceptions exist.

7.3 Access Provisioning

Access shall be provisioned only upon receipt of a formal, approved access request. The request must specify the systems, data, and level of access required, and must be approved by the user's manager or the data owner. IT shall process approved requests within [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] business days.

7.4 Access Deprovisioning

Access shall be revoked immediately upon termination of employment or contract. For role changes or transfers, access associated with the previous role shall be revoked within one (1) business day, and new access shall be provisioned per the standard request process. HR must notify IT of all terminations and transfers on or before the effective date.

7.5 Quarterly Access Reviews

Access reviews shall be conducted at least quarterly for all systems classified as Confidential or Restricted. Reviews shall be performed by data owners or their designated delegates. Reviewers shall verify that each user's access is appropriate for their current role. Identified discrepancies must be remediated within five (5) business days of discovery.

7.6 Privileged Access Management

Privileged accounts shall be assigned to named individuals — shared administrator accounts are prohibited. Privileged access shall require enhanced authentication (multi-factor authentication is mandatory). All privileged access activity shall be logged and monitored. Privileged access shall be reviewed monthly. Administrative access to production systems must be justified and time-bound wherever technically feasible.

7.7 Emergency Access

Emergency access procedures shall be documented and available to authorized personnel. Emergency access shall be granted only when normal provisioning processes cannot meet urgent business or operational needs. All emergency access grants must be logged, reviewed within 24 hours, and formally approved or revoked. A post-incident review shall assess whether the emergency access was appropriate.

7.8 Service Accounts

Service accounts shall have a designated owner responsible for their security. Credentials for service accounts must be stored securely (e.g., in a secrets management system) and rotated at least every [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] days. Service accounts shall not be used for interactive login by humans.

7.9 Remote Access

Remote access to Ramdoot Solutions systems shall be permitted only through approved, encrypted channels (VPN or equivalent). Remote access requires multi-factor authentication. Devices used for remote access must comply with Ramdoot Solutions endpoint security requirements.

8. Detailed Requirements

8.1 User Account Management

  • Each user shall have a unique, individually assigned account. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited for interactive use.
  • Accounts not used for [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] consecutive days shall be automatically disabled.
  • Default vendor accounts shall be disabled or renamed before systems are placed into production.

8.2 Access Request Workflow

  1. User or manager submits a formal access request specifying systems, role, and business justification.
  2. Data owner or manager reviews and approves or rejects the request.
  3. IT provisions the access and notifies the requester.
  4. The access grant is recorded in the access management log.

8.3 Segregation of Duties

Access rights shall be structured to enforce segregation of duties. No single individual shall have access that allows them to execute conflicting transactions without independent review (e.g., requesting and approving their own access, developing and deploying code to production without review).

8.4 Physical Access Controls

Physical access to server rooms, network closets, and other sensitive areas shall be restricted to authorized personnel. Physical access logs shall be maintained and reviewed quarterly.

9. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy must be documented with a business justification and risk assessment, approved by the Information Security Officer, and recorded in the exception register. Exceptions shall be time-bound (maximum 12 months), subject to compensating controls, and reviewed quarterly.

10. Monitoring and Compliance

  • Quarterly access reviews shall produce documented evidence of review outcomes and remediation actions.
  • Privileged access shall be monitored continuously; anomalous activity shall trigger alerts.
  • Annual internal audits shall assess compliance with this policy, including testing of provisioning, deprovisioning, and review processes.
  • Metrics shall be tracked, including: time to provision, time to deprovision, number of orphaned accounts, and access review completion rates.

11. Violations and Enforcement

Violations of this policy, including sharing credentials, accessing systems without authorization, or circumventing access controls, may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract. Violations shall be reported to the Information Security Officer and investigated promptly. Ramdoot Solutions reserves the right to pursue legal action where warranted.

12. Records and Evidence

  • Access request forms and approval records.
  • Quarterly access review reports and sign-off records.
  • Privileged access activity logs.
  • Emergency access logs and post-incident reviews.
  • Provisioning and deprovisioning audit trails.
  • Role definitions and RBAC mapping documents.
  • Exception request forms and approvals.

Records shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention Policy (RS-POL-007).

  • Changes in regulatory or compliance requirements.
  • Findings from internal or external audits.
