1. Purpose

This policy establishes a structured framework for identifying, assessing, treating, and monitoring information security risks across Ramdoot Solutions. It ensures that risk management activities are consistent, repeatable, and aligned with the organization's strategic objectives and risk appetite.

Effective risk management enables Ramdoot Solutions to make informed decisions about resource allocation, control implementation, and risk acceptance while maintaining compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC3.1–CC3.4, CC9.1) and NISP requirements.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All information assets, systems, processes, and services operated by or on behalf of Ramdoot Solutions
  • All business functions and departments
  • All employees, contractors, and third-party service providers
  • All locations from which Ramdoot Solutions operates, including remote work environments
  • Risks arising from internal operations, external threats, regulatory changes, and third-party relationships

3. Objectives

  • Establish a consistent methodology for risk identification, assessment, and treatment
  • Define and communicate Ramdoot Solutions' risk appetite and tolerance levels
  • Maintain a comprehensive risk register that is reviewed and updated regularly
  • Ensure risks are treated to levels within the organization's defined appetite
  • Provide management with visibility into the organization's risk posture through regular reporting
  • Support compliance with SOC 2, NISP, and other applicable frameworks

4. Definitions

TermDefinition
RiskThe potential for an event or action to adversely affect the organization's ability to achieve its objectives, expressed as a function of likelihood and impact.
ThreatAny circumstance or event with the potential to cause harm to an information asset.
VulnerabilityA weakness in an asset or control that can be exploited by a threat.
LikelihoodThe probability that a threat will exploit a vulnerability, rated on a defined scale.
ImpactThe consequence or damage that would result from a risk being realized, rated on a defined scale.
Risk AppetiteThe level and type of risk the organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives.
Risk ToleranceThe acceptable variation in outcomes relative to achieving objectives.
Risk RegisterA structured record of identified risks, their assessments, treatment plans, owners, and status.
Residual RiskThe risk remaining after treatment measures have been applied.
Inherent RiskThe risk level before any controls or mitigations are applied.

6. Policy Statements

  1. Ramdoot Solutions shall maintain a formal risk management framework that is integrated into its governance and decision-making processes.
  2. A comprehensive risk assessment must be conducted at least annually and whenever significant changes occur to the business environment, technology, or threat landscape.
  3. All identified risks shall be documented in the risk register with assigned owners, risk ratings, treatment plans, and target dates.
  4. Risk treatment decisions must align with the organization's defined risk appetite and be approved at the appropriate authority level.
  5. Residual risks that exceed the organization's risk tolerance shall be escalated to senior management for formal acceptance or additional treatment.
  6. Risk management activities must be documented and auditable to support SOC 2 and NISP compliance requirements.
  7. Management shall receive quarterly risk reports summarizing the organization's risk posture, treatment progress, and emerging risks.
  8. Third-party risks shall be assessed and managed in accordance with RS-POL-018 (Vendor Management Policy).

7. Detailed Requirements

7.1 Risk Management Framework

Ramdoot Solutions' risk management framework shall follow a continuous cycle of:

  1. Establish Context: Define the internal and external context, risk criteria, and risk appetite.
  2. Risk Identification: Systematically identify risks to information assets, processes, and services.
  3. Risk Analysis: Determine the likelihood and impact of each identified risk.
  4. Risk Evaluation: Compare risk levels against risk criteria and prioritize for treatment.
  5. Risk Treatment: Select and implement treatment options for risks requiring action.
  6. Monitoring & Review: Continuously monitor risks, controls, and the effectiveness of treatment plans.
  7. Communication & Reporting: Report risk information to stakeholders at appropriate levels.

7.3 Risk Appetite Statement

Risk Appetite. Ramdoot Solutions adopts a conservative risk appetite regarding information security and data protection. The organization will not accept risks rated as Critical. High-rated risks must have active treatment plans with target dates. Medium risks shall be monitored and treated where cost-effective. Low risks may be accepted with documented justification.

7.4 Risk Register Maintenance

  • The risk register shall be the single authoritative source for all identified information security risks.
  • Each risk entry must include: risk ID, description, threat/vulnerability, affected assets, inherent risk rating, existing controls, residual risk rating, treatment plan, risk owner, target date, and status.
  • The risk register shall be reviewed and updated at least quarterly by the Information Security Officer.
  • New risks identified through incidents, audits, or assessments must be added to the register within 5 business days.
  • Closed risks shall be archived but retained for audit and trend analysis purposes.

7.5 Risk Treatment Options

Treatment OptionDescriptionWhen to Apply
MitigateImplement controls to reduce likelihood, impact, or bothWhen cost-effective controls are available and the risk exceeds appetite
AcceptAcknowledge and retain the risk without additional treatmentWhen the risk is within appetite; requires formal documentation and approval
TransferShare the risk with a third party (e.g., insurance, outsourcing)When financial impact is the primary concern and transfer mechanisms are available
AvoidEliminate the risk by discontinuing the activity or removing the assetWhen the risk is unacceptable and no cost-effective mitigation exists

7.6 Annual Risk Assessment

  • A comprehensive risk assessment shall be conducted annually, coordinated by the Information Security Officer.
  • The annual assessment must include workshops with department heads and key system owners.
  • External threat intelligence and industry-specific risk trends shall be incorporated into the assessment.
  • Results must be presented to senior management within 30 days of assessment completion.
  • Ad-hoc risk assessments shall be conducted when triggered by: major system changes, security incidents, new business initiatives, regulatory changes, or new vendor engagements.

7.7 Management Reporting

  • Quarterly risk reports shall be presented to senior management, including: risk register summary, treatment plan status, new and closed risks, top risks by severity, and emerging risk trends.
  • Critical and high risks must be reported to management immediately upon identification.
  • An annual risk management report shall provide a comprehensive overview of the organization's risk posture, framework effectiveness, and recommendations for improvement.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally documented and approved. Each exception shall include:

  • Justification and business rationale
  • Assessment of additional risk introduced by the exception
  • Compensating controls
  • Time-bound duration (maximum 12 months)

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • The risk management framework shall be independently reviewed at least annually to assess its effectiveness and alignment with organizational objectives.
  • Key performance indicators include: risk register completeness, treatment plan completion rate, overdue treatment actions, and accuracy of risk ratings.
  • Internal audits shall verify that risk assessments are conducted as scheduled and that treatment plans are implemented as documented.
  • Compliance with this policy must be included in the scope of SOC 2 and NISP audits.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Formal counselling and retraining on risk management responsibilities
  • Reassignment of risk ownership for unmanaged risks
  • Disciplinary action for deliberate circumvention of risk management processes
  • Escalation to senior management for systemic non-compliance
