1. Purpose

This policy establishes the principles, requirements, and controls for the protection of personal data processed by Ramdoot Solutions. It ensures that the organization respects the privacy rights of individuals, complies with applicable Indian data protection legislation — including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 — and meets the requirements of SOC 2 Privacy criteria and NISP data protection controls.

This policy demonstrates Ramdoot Solutions' commitment to handling personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All personal data processed by Ramdoot Solutions, whether in electronic or physical form
  • Personal data of employees, contractors, clients, vendors, and any other individuals
  • All systems, applications, and processes that collect, store, process, or transmit personal data
  • All employees, contractors, and third parties who handle personal data on behalf of Ramdoot Solutions
  • Data processing activities conducted within India and any cross-border transfers

3. Objectives

  • Define what constitutes personal data and sensitive personal data within the organization's context
  • Establish lawful bases for processing personal data in alignment with the DPDP Act 2023
  • Protect the rights of data principals (data subjects) and provide mechanisms for exercising those rights
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data
  • Ensure compliance with cross-border data transfer requirements
  • Establish clear procedures for data breach notification
  • Embed privacy considerations into business processes through Privacy Impact Assessments

4. Definitions

TermDefinition
Personal DataAny data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data (as defined under DPDP Act 2023). Includes name, email, phone number, address, employee ID, IP address, and similar identifiers.
Sensitive Personal DataPersonal data that reveals financial data, health data, biometric data, genetic data, caste, religious belief, or any data designated as sensitive by the Central Government.
Data PrincipalThe individual to whom personal data relates (equivalent to "data subject" under GDPR).
Data FiduciaryThe entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal data. Ramdoot Solutions acts as a Data Fiduciary for employee and client data.
Data ProcessorAn entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.
ProcessingAny operation performed on personal data, including collection, recording, storage, use, disclosure, sharing, erasure, or destruction.
ConsentA free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous indication of the Data Principal's wishes, by a clear affirmative action, signifying agreement to the processing of their personal data.
Data BreachAny unauthorized or accidental access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction of personal data.
Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)A systematic process for evaluating the potential effects on privacy of a project, initiative, or system.
Cross-Border TransferThe transfer of personal data to a jurisdiction outside India.

6. Policy Statements

  1. Ramdoot Solutions shall process personal data only for lawful, specific, and clearly stated purposes communicated to the Data Principal.
  2. Personal data must be collected with valid consent or another lawful basis as permitted under the DPDP Act 2023.
  3. The organization shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
  4. Data Principals' rights — including the right to access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal — must be facilitated promptly and transparently.
  5. Cross-border transfers of personal data shall only occur to jurisdictions not restricted by the Central Government, and with appropriate safeguards in place.
  6. Data breaches involving personal data must be reported to the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals as required by law.
  7. Privacy Impact Assessments shall be conducted for new systems, processes, or projects that involve significant processing of personal data.
  8. Personal data must not be retained longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless required by law.

7.4 Consent Management

  • Consent shall be obtained through clear, affirmative action — pre-ticked boxes or implied consent are not acceptable.
  • Consent notices must be in clear, plain language and specify: the data being collected, the purpose of processing, the Data Fiduciary's identity, and the right to withdraw consent.
  • Consent shall be as easy to withdraw as it is to give. Withdrawal mechanisms must be prominently accessible.
  • Records of consent (date, method, scope) must be maintained and auditable.
  • For processing children's data (under 18 years), verifiable consent from the parent or lawful guardian shall be obtained.

7.5 Cross-Border Transfers

  • Personal data shall not be transferred to any country or territory restricted by the Central Government under the DPDP Act 2023.
  • Before any cross-border transfer, Ramdoot Solutions must verify that the destination country is not on the restricted list maintained by the Government of India.
  • Appropriate contractual safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses, Data Processing Agreements) shall be in place before any transfer.
  • A record of all cross-border data transfers must be maintained, including: data categories, destination country, recipient, purpose, and safeguards applied.

7.6 Data Breach Notification

Mandatory Breach Notification. Under the DPDP Act 2023, every personal data breach must be notified to the Data Protection Board of India and each affected Data Principal. Failure to do so may result in significant financial penalties.

  1. Detection: All employees must report suspected personal data breaches immediately to the Information Security Officer and Privacy Lead.
  2. Assessment: The Privacy Lead shall assess the breach within 24 hours to determine scope, severity, and affected Data Principals.
  3. Notification to Board: The Data Protection Board of India shall be notified in the prescribed manner and timeframe as specified under the DPDP Act 2023.
  4. Notification to Data Principals: Affected Data Principals must be notified with details of the breach and remedial actions taken.
  5. Containment & Remediation: Immediate steps shall be taken to contain the breach, prevent recurrence, and remediate affected systems.
  6. Documentation: All breach details, timelines, decisions, notifications, and remedial actions must be documented and retained for 5 years.

7.7 DPDP Act 2023 Alignment

Ramdoot Solutions shall ensure ongoing alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including:

  • Appointing a designated contact person to respond to Data Protection Board inquiries
  • Maintaining a clear and accessible privacy notice in English and Hindi
  • Implementing reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches
  • Conducting periodic compliance audits against DPDP Act requirements
  • Monitoring for rules, regulations, and notifications issued by the Central Government under the Act
  • If designated as a Significant Data Fiduciary: appointing an independent Data Auditor, conducting periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments, and appointing a Data Protection Officer resident in India

7.8 Privacy Impact Assessments (PIA)

A PIA shall be conducted in the following circumstances:

  • Before launching a new system or application that processes personal data
  • Before introducing a new data processing activity or changing an existing one significantly
  • Before engaging a new vendor or data processor for personal data processing
  • When implementing new technologies (e.g., AI, biometrics, automated decision-making)

The PIA must document: data flows, risks to Data Principals, proposed mitigations, and residual risks. PIA results shall be reviewed and approved by the Privacy Lead before the processing activity commences.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy are not permitted for statutory obligations under the DPDP Act 2023. For operational requirements that may deviate from policy standards, exceptions must be formally requested and include:

  • Detailed justification and business need
  • Assessment of privacy risks introduced
  • Compensating controls
  • Time-bound duration (maximum 6 months before re-evaluation)

Exceptions shall be approved by the Privacy Lead and [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • The Privacy Lead shall conduct annual privacy compliance audits covering data processing activities, consent records, and breach response readiness.
  • Data subject request response times must be tracked and reported quarterly.
  • Privacy awareness training completion rates shall be monitored (target: 100% of employees annually).
  • PIA completion status for new processing activities must be tracked.
  • Compliance with the DPDP Act 2023 shall be verified through periodic legal review.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Mandatory retraining on data protection requirements
  • Formal disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment
  • Contractual penalties for third parties who violate data protection requirements
  • Regulatory penalties under the DPDP Act 2023 (up to INR 250 crore for certain violations)
  • Civil liability for damages caused by unauthorized processing or breach
