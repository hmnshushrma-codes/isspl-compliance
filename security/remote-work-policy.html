1. Purpose

This policy defines the security requirements and controls for employees and contractors working remotely (from home, co-working spaces, client sites, or while travelling). It ensures that remote work arrangements maintain the same level of information security as on-premises operations.

As Ramdoot Solutions supports flexible work arrangements, this policy ensures that remote access to organizational resources does not introduce unacceptable security risks, in compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC6.6) and NISP remote access requirements.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All Ramdoot Solutions employees and contractors who work remotely, whether full-time, part-time, or occasionally
  • All devices — company-issued and approved personal devices — used to access organizational systems and data from remote locations
  • All remote access methods including VPN, cloud-based applications, and remote desktop services
  • All locations outside Ramdoot Solutions' controlled office premises, including home offices, co-working spaces, client sites, public locations, and travel

3. Objectives

  • Ensure secure connectivity between remote workers and organizational resources
  • Protect organizational data on devices and networks outside the corporate perimeter
  • Define acceptable tools, platforms, and practices for remote collaboration
  • Mitigate risks specific to remote work environments (unsecured networks, shared spaces, physical theft)
  • Maintain compliance with SOC 2 CC6.6 and NISP requirements for remote access security

6. Policy Statements

  1. All remote access to Ramdoot Solutions' internal systems shall use the organization's approved VPN with full-tunnel encryption.
  2. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for all remote access to organizational systems and cloud services.
  3. Remote workers shall ensure their home network meets the minimum security requirements defined in this policy.
  4. All devices used for remote work must comply with the organization's device security standards, including encryption, screen lock, and current antivirus.
  5. Split tunnelling shall not be permitted on VPN connections.
  6. Organizational data must not be stored on unapproved personal devices or cloud services.
  7. Remote workers shall use only approved collaboration tools for business communications.
  8. Physical documents containing confidential information must be secured at the remote work location and not left visible or accessible to unauthorized persons.

7. Detailed Requirements

7.1 VPN Requirements

  • All remote access to internal networks, databases, and administrative interfaces shall be conducted through the organization's approved VPN.
  • The VPN must use strong encryption (AES-256 or equivalent) with secure protocols (WireGuard, OpenVPN, or IPSec).
  • Split tunnelling shall not be enabled — all traffic must route through the VPN when connected.
  • VPN sessions shall automatically disconnect after [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] minutes of inactivity.
  • VPN access credentials must not be shared with any other person, including family members.
  • VPN client software shall be kept up to date with the latest security patches.
  • Access to the VPN from untrusted or public computers (e.g., internet cafes, hotel business centres) is prohibited.

7.2 Home Network Security

Remote workers shall ensure their home network meets the following minimum requirements:

  • Wi-Fi networks must use WPA3 or WPA2 encryption — WEP and open networks are prohibited.
  • The default router administrator password shall be changed from the factory default to a strong, unique password.
  • Router firmware should be updated to the latest available version.
  • The Wi-Fi network name (SSID) should not reveal the employee's name or organization.
  • Where possible, a dedicated Wi-Fi network or VLAN should be used to separate work devices from personal and IoT devices.
  • Remote workers shall complete a home network security self-assessment checklist annually, provided by the IT team.

7.4 Screen Lock & Visual Privacy

  • Devices shall be configured to automatically lock after a maximum of 5 minutes of inactivity.
  • Employees must manually lock their screen (Win+L / Ctrl+Cmd+Q) whenever stepping away from their device, even briefly.
  • When working in shared or public spaces, employees shall use a privacy screen filter to prevent visual eavesdropping.
  • Video calls must be conducted in a private area where conversations cannot be overheard by unauthorized persons. Virtual backgrounds should be used when the physical background may reveal sensitive information.

7.5 Data Handling on Personal Devices

  • Organizational data shall not be downloaded or stored locally on personal devices unless the device is enrolled in the approved BYOD programme.
  • Confidential data must not be transferred to personal USB drives, personal cloud storage, or personal email accounts.
  • If BYOD is approved, organizational data shall be segregated from personal data using a managed container or workspace application.
  • Upon termination of the remote work arrangement or employment, all organizational data on personal devices must be securely removed within 24 hours.
  • Personal devices used for BYOD must consent to remote wipe of the managed workspace/container.

7.7 Physical Document Security at Home

  • Printing of confidential documents at home should be avoided wherever possible — digital formats are preferred.
  • If printed, confidential documents must be stored in a locked drawer or cabinet when not in active use.
  • Confidential documents shall not be disposed of in household waste — they must be shredded using a cross-cut shredder or returned to the office for secure destruction.
  • Remote workers must not leave confidential documents visible or accessible to family members, housemates, or visitors.
  • At the end of a remote work arrangement, all physical documents containing organizational information shall be returned to the office or securely destroyed.

7.8 Public/Shared Workspaces & Travel

  • When working from co-working spaces, cafes, or while travelling, employees must use the VPN for all network activity.
  • Employees shall not connect to open/unsecured Wi-Fi networks — mobile hotspot is preferred over untrusted public Wi-Fi.
  • Devices must not be left unattended in public spaces, even briefly.
  • Sensitive phone calls and video conferences shall not be conducted in public spaces where conversations can be overheard.
  • Employees should enable device tracking (Find My Device) and have remote wipe capability active while travelling.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally requested and approved. Each request shall include:

  • Justification for the exception and specific requirement being waived
  • Risk assessment of the security implications
  • Compensating controls to be implemented
  • Duration of the exception (maximum 6 months before re-evaluation)

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • VPN access logs shall be monitored for anomalous activity (unusual hours, geographic anomalies, excessive failed attempts).
  • Device compliance must be verified through endpoint management tools, with non-compliant devices blocked from accessing organizational resources.
  • Home network security self-assessments shall be collected and reviewed annually.
  • Remote work security awareness must be included in the annual security training programme.
  • The Information Security Officer shall conduct periodic audits of remote work compliance.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Immediate suspension of remote access privileges pending investigation
  • Mandatory security retraining
  • Revocation of remote work privileges for repeated violations
  • Formal disciplinary action, up to and including termination, for deliberate or serious violations that result in data exposure
