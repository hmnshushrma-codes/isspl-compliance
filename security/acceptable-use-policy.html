2. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the standards for responsible and appropriate use of Ramdoot Solutions information technology resources, including computers, networks, internet access, email, software, and data. The policy aims to protect the organization, its employees, and its clients from risks arising from improper use of IT assets.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and any other individuals authorized to use Ramdoot Solutions IT resources.
  • All IT resources owned, leased, or managed by Ramdoot Solutions, including hardware, software, networks, cloud services, email systems, and communication tools.
  • Personal devices used to access Ramdoot Solutions systems or data (Bring Your Own Device — BYOD).
  • All locations from which Ramdoot Solutions resources are accessed, including offices and remote work environments.

4. Objectives

  1. Establish clear expectations for the acceptable and prohibited use of Ramdoot Solutions IT resources.
  2. Protect the organization from legal liability, security incidents, and reputational harm resulting from misuse.
  3. Ensure that IT resources are used efficiently and primarily for legitimate business purposes.
  4. Promote user awareness of their responsibilities when using company IT assets.
  5. Establish a basis for enforcement actions when misuse occurs.

6. Roles and Responsibilities

6.1 Information Security Officer

The ISO shall maintain this policy, investigate reported violations, and recommend enforcement actions.

6.2 IT Department

IT shall implement technical controls to enforce this policy (web filtering, email security, endpoint protection), monitor usage patterns, and support investigations into suspected misuse.

6.3 Department Managers

Managers shall ensure their team members understand and comply with this policy. They shall report suspected violations and support enforcement actions as necessary.

6.4 Human Resources

HR shall ensure this policy is communicated to all employees during onboarding and shall coordinate disciplinary actions for violations.

6.5 All Users

Every individual with access to Ramdoot Solutions IT resources is responsible for reading, understanding, and complying with this policy. Users shall exercise good judgment and report policy violations or suspicious activities.

7. Policy Statements

7.1 General Acceptable Use

Ramdoot Solutions IT resources are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with job performance, consume excessive resources, violate any policy, or expose the organization to risk. All users shall exercise reasonable care when using IT resources.

7.2 Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Ramdoot Solutions IT resources:

  • Accessing, downloading, storing, or distributing illegal, obscene, discriminatory, harassing, or threatening material.
  • Unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or data — including attempts to circumvent security controls.
  • Installing unauthorized software, applications, or browser extensions without IT approval.
  • Using IT resources for personal commercial activities, gambling, or cryptocurrency mining.
  • Sending unsolicited bulk email (spam) or chain letters from company systems.
  • Misrepresenting one's identity or impersonating another person in electronic communications.
  • Connecting unauthorized devices to the Ramdoot Solutions network.
  • Intentionally introducing malware, viruses, or malicious code.
  • Violating software licensing agreements or intellectual property rights.
  • Exfiltrating or unauthorized transfer of company or client data to external systems or personal accounts.

7.3 Internet Usage

Internet access is provided for business purposes. Users shall not access websites that are illegal, malicious, or inappropriate for the workplace. Ramdoot Solutions reserves the right to monitor internet usage and implement web content filtering. Downloads from the internet shall only be from trusted, verified sources.

7.4 Email and Communication

Company email shall be used in a professional manner. Users shall not use company email for unauthorized bulk messaging, forwarding of chain letters, or distribution of inappropriate content. Confidential or restricted information shall only be emailed using approved encryption methods. Users shall exercise caution with attachments and links to avoid phishing attacks.

7.5 Personal Devices (BYOD)

Personal devices used to access Ramdoot Solutions systems or data must comply with the following:

  • Devices must have up-to-date operating systems and security patches.
  • Devices must have active antivirus/anti-malware software.
  • Devices must have screen lock enabled with a minimum PIN/password.
  • Company data on personal devices must be stored in approved, encrypted containers or applications.
  • Ramdoot Solutions reserves the right to remotely wipe company data from personal devices if lost, stolen, or upon termination.

7.6 Social Media

Employees shall not disclose confidential or proprietary information on social media platforms. When posting in a professional capacity or mentioning Ramdoot Solutions, employees shall follow guidelines issued by [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS]. Personal social media use during work hours must be minimal and shall not interfere with productivity.

7.7 Software Installation

Only software approved by the IT department shall be installed on Ramdoot Solutions devices. Users shall not install peer-to-peer file sharing software, remote access tools, or any software from untrusted sources. Requests for new software shall be submitted through the IT service desk for evaluation and approval. Shadow IT is prohibited.

7.8 Data Handling

Users shall handle data in accordance with the Data Classification Policy (RS-POL-006). Sensitive data shall not be stored on local devices unless encrypted. Data shall not be transferred to unauthorized cloud storage, personal email accounts, or removable media without approval. Printed confidential documents shall be collected promptly and stored securely.

7.9 Monitoring and Privacy

Ramdoot Solutions reserves the right to monitor, log, and audit the use of its IT resources without prior notice, to the extent permitted by applicable Indian law. Users shall have no expectation of privacy when using company IT resources. Monitoring may include internet usage, email content, file access, and system activity logs.

8. Detailed Requirements

8.1 Clean Desk and Screen

Users shall lock their workstations when stepping away. Sensitive documents shall not be left visible on desks or screens. Whiteboards containing sensitive information shall be erased after meetings.

8.2 Removable Media

Use of removable media (USB drives, external hard drives, SD cards) shall be restricted. Where permitted, removable media containing Ramdoot Solutions data must be encrypted. The use of personal removable media on company systems is prohibited unless authorized by IT.

8.3 Remote Work

Employees working remotely shall ensure their workspace is secure, use VPN for all access to company systems, and prevent unauthorized individuals from viewing screens or overhearing confidential conversations. Home Wi-Fi networks shall use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption.

8.4 Reporting Obligations

Users must report the following events to IT or the ISO immediately: loss or theft of devices containing company data, suspected security incidents, phishing attempts, unauthorized access, and any observed policy violations by others.

9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written justification, a risk assessment, and approval from the Information Security Officer. All approved exceptions shall be documented, time-bound (maximum 12 months), and subject to compensating controls. The exception register shall be reviewed quarterly.

10. Monitoring and Compliance

  • IT shall implement automated monitoring tools for internet usage, email, and endpoint activities.
  • Periodic audits shall be conducted to verify compliance with software installation, BYOD, and data handling requirements.
  • Security awareness training shall include acceptable use topics and shall be conducted annually.
  • Reports on policy violations and trends shall be presented to management quarterly.

11. Violations and Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in progressive disciplinary action, including verbal warning, written warning, suspension of IT access, termination of employment or contract, and legal action where warranted. The severity of the response shall be proportionate to the nature and impact of the violation. Ramdoot Solutions may refer criminal activity to law enforcement authorities.

12. Records and Evidence

  • Signed policy acknowledgment forms from all users.
  • Security awareness training attendance records.
  • Internet usage and email monitoring logs.
  • Software inventory and approval records.
  • BYOD registration records.
  • Violation investigation reports and disciplinary records.
  • Exception request forms and approvals.

Records shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention Policy (RS-POL-007).

15. Review Cycle

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually, or sooner upon:

  • Introduction of new technologies or communication platforms.
  • Significant policy violations that reveal gaps.
  • Changes in applicable laws or regulations.
  • Feedback from employees or audit findings.
