1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements and procedures for securely offboarding employees, contractors, and other personnel when they leave Ramdoot Solutions — whether through resignation, termination, contract completion, or other circumstances. It ensures that all access rights are revoked, organizational assets are returned, and knowledge is appropriately transferred to prevent security gaps and data loss.

Timely and thorough offboarding is critical to protecting Ramdoot Solutions' information assets and maintaining compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC6.2, CC6.3) and NISP access control requirements.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All departing employees, regardless of reason for departure (resignation, termination, retirement, redundancy)
  • All departing contractors, consultants, and temporary workers
  • Interns and trainees completing their engagement
  • Employees transferring between departments or roles (partial offboarding for access adjustment)
  • All systems, accounts, physical assets, and data access associated with the departing individual

3. Objectives

  • Revoke all access rights on the same day as departure to prevent unauthorized post-employment access
  • Recover all organizational assets (devices, badges, documents, keys) before or on the last working day
  • Conduct knowledge transfer to ensure business continuity
  • Handle email and account data in accordance with organizational and legal requirements
  • Revoke digital certificates, API keys, and cryptographic credentials
  • Perform a final access audit to verify complete access removal
  • Document the offboarding process for compliance and audit purposes

4. Definitions

TermDefinition
OffboardingThe process of managing the departure of an employee or contractor, including access revocation, asset return, and knowledge transfer.
Access RevocationThe process of disabling or removing a departing individual's access to all organizational systems, applications, and facilities.
Exit InterviewA structured interview conducted with the departing individual to gather feedback, remind them of ongoing obligations, and confirm completion of offboarding tasks.
Knowledge TransferThe process of documenting and transferring job-related knowledge, processes, and information from the departing individual to their successor or team.
Same-Day RevocationThe requirement that all access rights be revoked on the individual's last working day (or immediately in cases of involuntary termination).
Final Access AuditA post-offboarding verification that all access has been completely removed across all systems.
Involuntary TerminationDeparture initiated by the organization (e.g., dismissal, redundancy) as opposed to voluntary resignation.

6. Policy Statements

  1. All access to Ramdoot Solutions' systems, applications, and facilities shall be revoked on the departing individual's last working day — or immediately in cases of involuntary termination for cause.
  2. An exit interview must be conducted with every departing employee and contractor, covering security obligations, NDA re-acknowledgement, and asset return.
  3. All organizational assets shall be returned before or on the last working day, using a documented asset return checklist.
  4. Knowledge transfer must be initiated at least [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] weeks before the departure date for voluntary resignations.
  5. Email accounts and cloud storage shall be handled according to the procedures in Section 7.5 to ensure no loss of business data.
  6. All digital certificates, API keys, SSH keys, and cryptographic credentials associated with the departing individual must be revoked or rotated on the departure date.
  7. A final access audit shall be completed within 5 business days of departure to verify that all access has been completely removed.
  8. The offboarding process must be fully documented and retained for audit purposes.

7.1.1 Systems Requiring Access Revocation

The following systems must be included in the access revocation process:

  • Active Directory / Identity Provider (SSO) — primary account deactivation
  • Email system (organizational email account)
  • VPN and remote access services
  • Cloud services and SaaS applications (Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, Jira, etc.)
  • Internal applications (CertFlow, CRM, ERP, etc.)
  • Database access and admin panels
  • Server access (SSH keys, RDP accounts)
  • Code repositories (GitHub, GitLab)
  • Cloud infrastructure consoles (AWS, GCP, Azure, VPS panels)
  • Physical access systems (badge, biometric)
  • Wi-Fi network access
  • Any shared or service accounts the individual had access to

7.2 Exit Interview

HR shall conduct an exit interview with every departing individual, covering:

  1. NDA Re-acknowledgement: Remind the departing individual of their ongoing confidentiality obligations under the NDA. Obtain a signed re-acknowledgement.
  2. Intellectual Property: Confirm that all organizational intellectual property has been returned and no copies are retained by the individual.
  3. Asset Return: Review the asset return checklist (Section 7.3) and confirm all items have been returned.
  4. Outstanding Access: Ask the departing individual to identify any systems, accounts, or access they may have that are not on the standard offboarding checklist.
  5. Feedback: Gather feedback on security practices and any concerns the individual may wish to raise.
  6. Contact Information: Collect forwarding contact information in case of follow-up questions.

7.4 Knowledge Transfer

  • The departing individual's manager shall initiate a knowledge transfer plan within 2 business days of receiving the resignation or termination notice.
  • The knowledge transfer must cover: current projects and their status, ongoing tasks and deadlines, critical processes owned by the individual, system administration credentials (to be transferred to a designated successor), client relationships and open items, and documentation of undocumented processes.
  • Knowledge transfer documentation shall be reviewed and approved by the manager before the individual's departure.
  • For involuntary terminations where knowledge transfer is not possible, the manager must assess and document the knowledge gap and create a remediation plan within 5 business days.

7.6 Certificate & Key Revocation

  • All SSH keys associated with the departing individual shall be removed from all servers and systems on the departure date.
  • Any personal TLS/SSL certificates issued to the individual must be revoked.
  • API keys and tokens created by the individual shall be rotated or revoked.
  • Code signing certificates associated with the individual must be revoked.
  • GPG/PGP keys used for organizational purposes shall be revoked from the organization's keyring.
  • Shared secrets or passwords known to the individual must be rotated within 24 hours of departure.

7.7 Final Access Audit

Within 5 business days of departure, IT shall complete a final access audit to verify:

  1. The primary identity account (Active Directory / IdP) is disabled and cannot authenticate.
  2. All application-specific accounts are disabled or removed.
  3. VPN access is revoked and cannot be re-established.
  4. Physical access badges and biometric data are deactivated and deleted.
  5. All SSH keys, certificates, and API tokens have been revoked or rotated.
  6. No orphaned accounts or access permissions remain.

The final access audit must be documented on the offboarding checklist and signed off by the IT lead and Information Security Officer.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy are extremely limited given the security implications of incomplete offboarding. Any exceptions must:

  • Be formally requested with detailed justification
  • Include a risk assessment and compensating controls
  • Be time-bound (maximum 30 days for access-related exceptions)
  • Be approved by the Information Security Officer and [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS]

No exception shall be granted that permits continued system access after the departure date without explicit, documented approval.

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • IT shall generate a monthly report of departures in the past 30 days cross-referenced against access revocation records to ensure 100% completion.
  • The Information Security Officer shall audit a sample of offboarding records quarterly to verify completeness.
  • Key metrics include: percentage of departures with same-day access revocation, average time to complete final access audit, asset return completion rate, and offboarding checklist completion rate.
  • Any access found active after the departure date must be treated as a security incident and investigated immediately.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Formal written warning for managers or IT personnel who fail to initiate or complete offboarding on time
  • Immediate remediation and incident reporting if access remains active post-departure
  • Disciplinary action for deliberate delays or obstruction of the offboarding process
  • Legal action against former personnel who retain unauthorized access or organizational data
