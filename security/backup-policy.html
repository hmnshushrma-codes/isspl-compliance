1. Purpose

This policy establishes the requirements for backup and recovery of Ramdoot Solutions' information systems, data, and configurations. It ensures that critical business data can be restored in a timely manner following data loss, corruption, hardware failure, or disaster events.

The policy supports the organization's business continuity objectives and ensures compliance with SOC 2 Availability criteria (A1.2) and NISP backup and recovery controls.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All production databases, including relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL datastores
  • Application source code repositories and build artifacts
  • System and application configuration files, including infrastructure-as-code definitions
  • Business documents, contracts, certificates, and compliance evidence
  • Email systems and collaboration platform data
  • User-generated content and uploaded files (e.g., MinIO/S3 object storage)
  • All environments: production, staging, and disaster recovery
  • All personnel responsible for system administration, DevOps, and IT operations at Ramdoot Solutions

3. Objectives

  • Ensure all critical data is backed up according to defined schedules and retention requirements
  • Minimize data loss by maintaining Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) appropriate to business criticality
  • Enable timely restoration of systems within defined Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
  • Protect backup data from unauthorized access, modification, and destruction
  • Validate backup integrity through regular testing and verification
  • Maintain compliance with SOC 2 A1.2 and NISP requirements

4. Definitions

TermDefinition
Full BackupA complete copy of all data within the defined backup scope at a specific point in time.
Incremental BackupA backup that captures only data that has changed since the last backup (full or incremental).
Differential BackupA backup that captures all data changed since the last full backup.
RPO (Recovery Point Objective)The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time.
RTO (Recovery Time Objective)The maximum acceptable time to restore a system or data after a disruption.
Offsite StorageA geographically separate location where backup copies are stored for disaster recovery purposes.
Backup EncryptionThe process of encoding backup data to prevent unauthorized access during storage and transit.
Retention PeriodThe duration for which backup copies must be maintained before they may be securely deleted.

6. Policy Statements

  1. All production systems and critical business data shall be backed up according to the schedules defined in this policy.
  2. Backup processes must be automated, monitored, and produce verifiable logs of success or failure.
  3. All backup media and data shall be encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent encryption, both at rest and in transit.
  4. Backup copies must be stored in at least two geographically separate locations.
  5. Backup integrity shall be verified through quarterly restore testing.
  6. Backup failures must be reported, investigated, and resolved within 24 hours.
  7. Access to backup systems and backup data shall be restricted to authorized personnel only, using the principle of least privilege.
  8. Backup retention periods must comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

7.4 Offsite Storage

  • Offsite backup copies shall be stored at a geographically separate facility, at least [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] kilometres from the primary data centre.
  • Cloud-based offsite storage must use services that comply with SOC 2 Type II requirements and provide data residency within India unless otherwise approved.
  • Transfer of backup data to offsite locations shall use encrypted channels (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • Offsite storage access must be restricted and audited with the same rigour as primary backup systems.

7.5 Backup Encryption

  • All backup data shall be encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Encryption keys must be managed separately from the backup data, following the organization's key management procedures.
  • Backup encryption keys shall be rotated annually or upon suspected compromise.
  • Key recovery procedures must be documented and tested to ensure backups can be decrypted when needed.

7.6 Integrity Testing

  • Backup integrity checks shall be performed on every backup job using checksums (SHA-256).
  • Quarterly restore tests must be conducted to verify that backup data can be successfully restored to a functional state.
  • Restore tests shall include at least one full database restore and one file-level restore per quarter.
  • Test results must be documented, including time to restore, data integrity verification, and any issues encountered.
  • Annual disaster recovery drills shall include a full system restore from offsite backups.

7.7 Restore Procedures

  1. Request: A restore request must be submitted via the IT service management system, specifying the data, system, and point-in-time required.
  2. Authorization: Restore requests shall be approved by the system owner and IT Operations lead before execution.
  3. Execution: The DevOps team shall perform the restore in an isolated environment first, verify data integrity, then apply to the target system.
  4. Verification: The requesting system owner must verify the restored data before the restore is considered complete.
  5. Documentation: All restore activities shall be logged, including requestor, approver, restore time, data restored, and outcome.

7.8 Monitoring & Alerting

  • Automated monitoring shall be configured for all backup jobs, with alerts sent on failure or warning conditions.
  • Backup job status must be reviewed by IT Operations daily.
  • A monthly backup compliance report shall be produced summarizing success rates, failures, storage utilization, and any deviations from schedule.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally requested using the Ramdoot Solutions exception request process. Each exception request shall include:

  • Justification for the exception
  • Risk assessment of granting the exception
  • Compensating controls to be implemented
  • Duration of the exception (maximum 12 months before re-evaluation)

Exceptions shall be approved by [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] and documented in the exception register.

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • The Information Security Officer shall audit backup processes at least annually to verify compliance with this policy.
  • Key metrics to be tracked include: backup success rate (target: 99.5%), restore test pass rate, RPO/RTO adherence, and encryption compliance.
  • Non-compliance findings must be reported to management and tracked to remediation.
  • Backup compliance status shall be included in quarterly management security reviews.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Verbal or written warning for first-time unintentional violations
  • Mandatory retraining on backup procedures
  • Formal disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, for repeated or deliberate violations
  • Civil or criminal liability if the violation results in data loss affecting clients or regulatory non-compliance

All violations shall be reported to the Information Security Officer and documented in the incident management system.
