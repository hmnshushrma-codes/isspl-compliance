2. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for the timely identification, evaluation, testing, and deployment of security and functional patches across all technology assets owned or managed by Ramdoot Solutions. Effective patch management reduces exposure to known vulnerabilities, maintains system stability, and supports regulatory and contractual compliance obligations.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All operating systems (Windows, Linux, macOS) deployed on servers, workstations, and mobile devices.
  • All commercial and open-source application software installed on Ramdoot Solutions systems.
  • Firmware on network devices (routers, switches, firewalls, load balancers, wireless access points).
  • Virtualization platforms and container runtimes (hypervisors, container engines, orchestration platforms).
  • Cloud-managed infrastructure components where Ramdoot Solutions retains patching responsibility.
  • Database management systems and middleware.
  • All employees, contractors, and third parties responsible for system administration or maintenance.

4. Objectives

  1. Ensure all systems are maintained at supported and patched versions to minimize vulnerability exposure.
  2. Establish consistent, repeatable processes for patch identification, testing, and deployment.
  3. Minimize service disruptions caused by patching through defined maintenance windows and testing procedures.
  4. Provide traceability and auditability of all patching activities.
  5. Align patch management practices with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and NISP requirements.

5. Definitions

TermDefinition
PatchA software update issued by a vendor to fix security vulnerabilities, bugs, or improve functionality.
HotfixAn urgent patch released outside the normal patch cycle to address a critical issue.
Service PackA cumulative collection of patches and updates bundled into a single release.
Firmware UpdateA software update applied to the embedded software of hardware devices.
Maintenance WindowA pre-approved time period during which system changes, including patch deployment, may be performed with minimal business impact.
RollbackThe process of reverting a system to its previous state after an unsuccessful or disruptive patch deployment.
Emergency PatchA patch deployed outside the normal schedule due to active exploitation or critical vulnerability severity.

7. Policy Statements

  1. Ramdoot Solutions shall maintain a formal patch management process that covers all in-scope technology assets.
  2. All security patches must be evaluated for applicability within 48 hours of vendor release.
  3. Critical and High-severity security patches shall be deployed within the remediation SLAs defined in the Vulnerability Management Policy (RS-POL-009).
  4. All patches must be tested in a non-production environment prior to production deployment, unless an emergency patch procedure is invoked.
  5. Patch deployment to production systems shall occur during approved maintenance windows, except for emergency patches.
  6. A rollback plan must be documented and validated before any patch is deployed to production.
  7. Systems that cannot be patched shall have compensating controls documented and approved through the risk acceptance process.
  8. End-of-life systems for which vendor patches are no longer available must be decommissioned or isolated with documented compensating controls within 90 days of end-of-support.
  9. Patch compliance status shall be tracked and reported monthly.

8. Detailed Requirements

8.1 Patch Classification

ClassificationDescriptionDeployment Timeline
Emergency / CriticalAddresses actively exploited vulnerabilities or CVSS 9.0+ severity; patch issued as a hotfix.Within 48 hours of validation
High PriorityAddresses CVSS 7.0 – 8.9 vulnerabilities; no known active exploitation.Within 7 calendar days
StandardAddresses CVSS 4.0 – 6.9 vulnerabilities or functional fixes.Within 30 calendar days (next scheduled maintenance window)
Low / RoutineMinor bug fixes, cosmetic updates, CVSS < 4.0 vulnerabilities.Within 90 calendar days (scheduled maintenance cycle)

8.2 Patch Testing Requirements

  1. All patches shall be tested in a non-production environment that mirrors the production configuration.
  2. Testing must include:
    • Verification that the patch installs successfully without errors.
    • Functional testing to confirm that existing application and system functionality is unaffected.
    • Compatibility testing with critical business applications.
    • Rollback testing to confirm the system can be reverted to its pre-patch state.
  3. Test results shall be documented and approved by the responsible system administrator before production deployment.
  4. For emergency patches where full testing is not feasible, abbreviated testing must be performed with documented justification, and a post-deployment validation shall be completed within 24 hours.

8.3 Deployment Windows

  • Standard maintenance windows shall be defined and communicated to all stakeholders. The default maintenance window is: [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].
  • Patches must be deployed in a phased approach (e.g., non-critical systems first, then critical systems) to limit blast radius.
  • Users and business units shall be notified at least 48 hours in advance of scheduled maintenance windows that may impact system availability.
  • Emergency patch deployments may occur outside standard windows with CISO or IT Operations Manager approval.

8.4 Emergency Patch Procedure

  1. The IT Security Team identifies an emergency patching requirement and notifies the IT Operations Manager and CISO.
  2. The CISO or delegate authorizes emergency deployment.
  3. Abbreviated testing shall be performed on at least one representative system.
  4. The patch is deployed with continuous monitoring during and after deployment.
  5. A post-deployment review must be conducted within 24 hours to confirm stability.
  6. An emergency change record shall be created retroactively and reviewed by the CAB at the next scheduled meeting.

8.5 Rollback Procedures

  • Before deploying any patch to production, a system backup or snapshot must be taken.
  • The rollback plan shall include step-by-step instructions for reverting the system to its pre-patch state.
  • Rollback must be executable within the maintenance window timeframe.
  • If a patch causes system instability or application failure, rollback shall be initiated immediately and the incident reported to the IT Security Team.

8.6 Compliance Tracking

  • Patch compliance shall be tracked using an automated patch management tool: [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].
  • A monthly Patch Compliance Report shall include:
    • Total number of applicable patches by classification.

9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be requested in writing and shall include:

  • The specific systems and patches affected.
  • The business or technical justification for the exception.
  • Compensating controls that mitigate the associated risk.
  • A proposed timeline for achieving full compliance.

Exceptions must be approved by the CISO and reviewed at least quarterly. All approved exceptions shall be recorded in the central exception register.

10. Monitoring & Compliance

  • Automated patch management tools shall be used to detect missing patches and report compliance status.
  • The IT Security Team shall conduct monthly patch compliance reviews.
  • Internal Audit shall include patch management effectiveness in its annual audit plan.
  • Key metrics include:
    • Patch SLA adherence rate by severity.
    • Mean time to patch by severity.
    • Number of systems running unsupported or end-of-life software.
    • Number of emergency patches deployed per quarter.
    • Rollback rate.

11. Violations & Enforcement

Non-compliance with this policy may result in:

  • Escalation to the CISO and the responsible business unit head.
  • Mandatory remediation with increased monitoring and reporting.
  • Restriction or isolation of non-compliant systems from the network.
  • Disciplinary action for individuals who willfully ignore patching requirements, up to and including termination.

15. Review Cycle

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually, or sooner if triggered by:

  • Significant changes to the organization's technology infrastructure.
  • Major patching failures or incidents resulting from unpatched systems.
  • Findings from internal or external audits.
  • Changes in vendor support or end-of-life status for critical systems.
