2. Purpose

This Information Security Policy establishes the strategic direction, principles, and governance framework for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all information assets owned, managed, or processed by Ramdoot Solutions. It serves as the master policy under which all subordinate security policies, standards, and procedures operate.

This policy exists to ensure that Ramdoot Solutions meets its obligations to clients, employees, partners, and regulators regarding the secure handling of information, while supporting business objectives and enabling operational efficiency.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All employees, contractors, consultants, temporary staff, and third parties who access Ramdoot Solutions information systems or data.
  • All information assets — whether digital, printed, or verbal — owned by or entrusted to Ramdoot Solutions.
  • All information systems, networks, applications, cloud services, and infrastructure operated by or on behalf of Ramdoot Solutions.
  • All locations from which Ramdoot Solutions business is conducted, including offices, remote work environments, and co-located facilities.

4. Objectives

  1. Protect the confidentiality of sensitive business, client, and employee information from unauthorized disclosure.
  2. Maintain the integrity of information and processing systems, ensuring data accuracy and completeness.
  3. Ensure availability of information systems and data to authorized users when required for business operations.
  4. Establish a governance structure for information security that assigns clear roles and accountability.
  5. Ensure compliance with applicable Indian laws, regulations, and contractual obligations.
  6. Promote a culture of security awareness across the organization.
  7. Enable continuous improvement through regular measurement, review, and adaptation of security controls.

6. Roles and Responsibilities

6.1 Senior Management

Senior management shall provide strategic direction, allocate adequate resources, and demonstrate visible commitment to information security. Management is responsible for approving this policy and ensuring its enforcement across the organization.

6.2 Information Security Officer (ISO)

The ISO shall be responsible for developing, implementing, and maintaining the information security management program. The ISO shall report to senior management on the effectiveness of security controls and recommend improvements. The ISO role shall be assigned to: [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].

6.3 Department Heads / Managers

Department heads shall ensure that all personnel within their teams understand and comply with this policy. They shall identify information assets under their control and ensure appropriate protections are in place.

6.4 IT Department

The IT department shall implement and maintain technical security controls, monitor systems for security events, manage access provisioning, and support incident response activities.

6.5 All Employees and Contractors

Every individual with access to Ramdoot Solutions information shall comply with this policy and all subordinate security policies. They shall report suspected security incidents promptly and complete required security awareness training.

6.6 Human Resources

HR shall ensure that security responsibilities are communicated during onboarding, that background checks are conducted as required, and that offboarding procedures include revocation of access rights.

7. Policy Statements

7.1 Information Security Governance

Ramdoot Solutions shall establish and maintain an information security governance structure that provides clear direction and oversight. Security objectives shall be aligned with business objectives and reviewed annually.

7.2 Information Classification

All information assets shall be classified according to the Data Classification Policy (RS-POL-006). Information shall be labeled, handled, stored, transmitted, and disposed of in accordance with its classification level. Four classification levels shall be maintained: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted.

7.3 Risk Management

Ramdoot Solutions shall conduct formal information security risk assessments at least annually and whenever significant changes occur to systems, processes, or the threat landscape. Risk treatment plans shall be documented, approved by management, and tracked to completion.

7.4 Access Control

Access to information and systems shall be granted on a need-to-know and least-privilege basis, as detailed in the Access Control Policy (RS-POL-002). All access must be authorized, authenticated, and auditable.

7.5 Acceptable Use

All users shall adhere to the Acceptable Use Policy (RS-POL-004). Use of Ramdoot Solutions information systems for illegal, unethical, or unauthorized purposes is strictly prohibited.

7.6 Security Awareness and Training

All employees and contractors shall complete security awareness training upon joining and at least annually thereafter. Targeted training shall be provided to personnel with elevated security responsibilities.

7.7 Incident Management

Ramdoot Solutions shall maintain incident response procedures to detect, report, assess, respond to, and recover from security incidents. All incidents shall be documented and analyzed for lessons learned.

7.8 Business Continuity

Information security considerations shall be integrated into business continuity and disaster recovery planning. Critical systems shall have defined recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs).

7.9 Compliance

Ramdoot Solutions shall comply with all applicable Indian laws and regulations, including but not limited to the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and any sector-specific regulations. Contractual security obligations to clients shall be identified and fulfilled.

8. Detailed Requirements

8.1 Security Controls

Ramdoot Solutions shall implement administrative, technical, and physical controls commensurate with the classification and value of information assets. Controls shall be selected based on risk assessment outcomes and shall be documented in a controls matrix.

8.2 Third-Party Security

Prior to granting third parties access to Ramdoot Solutions information or systems, a security assessment shall be conducted. Contracts with third parties must include appropriate security clauses, including data protection obligations, right-to-audit provisions, and incident notification requirements.

8.3 Encryption

Sensitive and restricted information shall be encrypted in transit and at rest, in accordance with the Encryption Policy (RS-POL-008). Only approved cryptographic algorithms and key lengths shall be used.

8.4 Change Management

Changes to information systems shall follow a documented change management process that includes security impact assessment, testing, approval, and rollback procedures.

8.5 Vulnerability Management

Ramdoot Solutions shall implement a vulnerability management program that includes regular scanning, timely patching, and tracking of remediation activities. Critical vulnerabilities must be remediated within [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] days of identification.

8.6 Logging and Monitoring

Security-relevant events shall be logged, retained, and monitored. Logs shall be protected from unauthorized access or modification and retained for a minimum period as defined in the Data Retention Policy (RS-POL-007).

9. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy must be formally requested in writing, include a business justification and risk assessment, and be approved by the Information Security Officer and relevant senior management. Approved exceptions shall be documented, time-bound (not to exceed 12 months), and subject to compensating controls. The exception register shall be reviewed quarterly.

10. Monitoring and Compliance

Compliance with this policy shall be monitored through:

  • Annual internal audits of information security controls and practices.
  • Periodic reviews of access rights, system configurations, and security logs.
  • Tracking of security metrics and key performance indicators (KPIs).
  • Management review meetings conducted at least annually.
  • Engagement of external auditors as required by contractual or regulatory obligations.

Non-compliance findings shall be documented, assigned to responsible owners, and tracked through to remediation.

11. Violations and Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract. Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, Ramdoot Solutions reserves the right to pursue legal remedies. Violations shall be reported to the Information Security Officer and investigated in accordance with applicable HR policies and Indian law.

Employees who become aware of policy violations shall report them through designated channels without fear of retaliation.

12. Records and Evidence

The following records shall be maintained as evidence of compliance:

  • Approved policy documents and version history.
  • Risk assessment reports and risk treatment plans.
  • Security awareness training records and attendance logs.
  • Internal and external audit reports.
  • Incident reports and post-incident reviews.
  • Exception request forms and approval records.
  • Management review meeting minutes.
  • Access review records.

Records shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention Policy (RS-POL-007).

15. Review Cycle

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or upon the occurrence of any of the following events:

  • Significant security incident or breach.
  • Material changes to the regulatory or legal environment.
  • Major changes to organizational structure, systems, or business processes.
  • Results of an internal or external audit that warrant policy updates.

The Information Security Officer shall initiate the review process and ensure updates are approved by senior management prior to publication.
