2. Purpose

This Data Classification Policy establishes a framework for categorizing information assets based on their sensitivity, value, and the impact of unauthorized disclosure, modification, or loss. Proper classification enables Ramdoot Solutions to apply proportionate security controls, ensuring that the most sensitive data receives the highest level of protection while avoiding unnecessary restrictions on lower-risk information.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All data and information created, received, stored, processed, or transmitted by Ramdoot Solutions, regardless of format (electronic, paper, verbal).
  • All employees, contractors, consultants, and third parties who handle Ramdoot Solutions data.
  • All systems, applications, and media that store or process Ramdoot Solutions data.

4. Objectives

  1. Define clear, actionable classification levels for all types of data.
  2. Establish handling requirements for each classification level covering storage, transmission, access, labeling, and disposal.
  3. Ensure data owners understand their responsibility to classify data and apply appropriate controls.
  4. Support compliance with the Information Technology Act, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and contractual obligations.
  5. Reduce the risk of data breaches by ensuring sensitive data is identifiable and adequately protected.

6. Roles and Responsibilities

6.1 Data Owners

Data owners shall classify data under their responsibility, ensure appropriate labeling, define access permissions, and initiate reclassification when the sensitivity or use of data changes.

6.2 Data Custodians

Data custodians shall implement the technical and procedural controls required for each classification level, including encryption, access controls, backup, and secure disposal.

6.3 Information Security Officer

The ISO shall define and maintain the classification framework, provide guidance on classification decisions, and audit compliance with classification and handling requirements.

6.4 All Employees and Contractors

All personnel shall handle data in accordance with its classification level, apply labels as required, and report misclassified or improperly handled data to the data owner or ISO.

7. Policy Statements

7.1 Classification Levels

Ramdoot Solutions adopts four classification levels. Data shall be classified into the highest applicable level.

7.1.1 Public

Information explicitly approved for unrestricted public access. Disclosure of Public data poses no risk to Ramdoot Solutions. Examples: published marketing materials, public website content, press releases.

7.1.2 Internal

Information intended for general use within Ramdoot Solutions but not approved for external distribution. Unauthorized disclosure could cause minor inconvenience. Examples: internal announcements, non-sensitive project documentation, internal policies (non-security), organizational charts.

7.1.3 Confidential

Sensitive business information whose disclosure could cause significant harm to Ramdoot Solutions, its clients, or partners. Access is restricted to authorized personnel with a demonstrated business need. Examples: client contracts, financial reports, employee personal data (PII), source code, security policies, audit reports, business strategies.

7.1.4 Restricted

The most sensitive category. Unauthorized disclosure could cause severe financial, legal, regulatory, or reputational harm. Access is strictly limited and individually approved. Examples: encryption keys, authentication credentials, sensitive personal data (health, financial, biometric), trade secrets, legal privilege communications, incident investigation details.

7.2 Default Classification

Data that has not yet been classified shall be treated as Confidential until the data owner assigns an appropriate classification level.

7.3 Reclassification

Data owners shall review classifications when the nature, use, or sensitivity of data changes. Reclassification requests shall be submitted to the ISO for validation. Reclassified data must be relabeled and its handling updated accordingly.

7.4 Labeling Requirements

All data and documents classified as Confidential or Restricted shall be clearly labeled with their classification level. Labeling requirements by format:

  • Electronic documents: Classification label in the document header or footer, and in the file metadata where supported.
  • Emails: Classification label in the subject line prefix (e.g., "[CONFIDENTIAL]") for Confidential and Restricted communications.
  • Physical documents: Classification stamp or label on the cover page and each subsequent page for Restricted documents.
  • Storage media: Physical label on the media indicating the highest classification level of data stored.
  • Systems/databases: Classification level documented in the CMDB asset record.

Public and Internal data need not be labeled unless required for clarity.

8.5 Printing

Confidential and Restricted documents shall be printed only when necessary. Print jobs for Restricted data shall use secure print (requiring user authentication at the printer). Printed copies shall be collected immediately and not left unattended. Surplus copies shall be shredded.

8.6 Verbal Disclosure

Confidential and Restricted information shall not be discussed in public areas, open offices where unauthorized persons may overhear, or on unsecured phone/video calls. Meeting rooms used for discussing Restricted information should be verified to be free of unauthorized recording devices.

9. Exceptions

Exceptions to classification or handling requirements must be approved by the data owner and the Information Security Officer, documented with a risk assessment and compensating controls, time-bound (maximum 12 months), and recorded in the exception register.

10. Monitoring and Compliance

  • Data classification audits shall be conducted at least annually to verify that data is appropriately classified and labeled.
  • Data loss prevention (DLP) tools shall be deployed to detect and prevent unauthorized transmission of Confidential and Restricted data.
  • Access logs for Restricted data shall be reviewed monthly.
  • Training on data classification and handling shall be included in annual security awareness programs.
  • Compliance with handling rules shall be verified during internal audits.

11. Violations and Enforcement

Misclassification, improper handling, unauthorized disclosure, or failure to label data as required by this policy constitutes a violation. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Unauthorized disclosure of Restricted data shall be treated as a security incident. Ramdoot Solutions may pursue legal remedies where warranted.

12. Records and Evidence

  • Data classification register / inventory.
  • Classification audit reports.
  • DLP monitoring reports and alerts.
  • Training records related to data classification.
  • Exception request forms and approvals.
  • Certificates of destruction for disposed Confidential/Restricted data.

Records shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention Policy (RS-POL-007).

15. Review Cycle

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually, or sooner upon:

  • Introduction of new data types or processing activities.
  • Changes in applicable data protection laws or regulations.
  • Data breach or classification-related incident.
  • Audit findings indicating gaps in classification practices.
