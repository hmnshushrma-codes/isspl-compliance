1. Purpose

This policy establishes the requirements for managing risks associated with third-party vendors, service providers, and suppliers that have access to Ramdoot Solutions' information, systems, or facilities. It ensures that vendor relationships are governed by appropriate security, privacy, and contractual controls throughout the vendor lifecycle.

Effective vendor management is essential to maintaining the trust of Ramdoot Solutions' clients and ensuring compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC9.1, CC9.2) and NISP third-party risk management requirements.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All third-party vendors, suppliers, contractors, and service providers engaged by Ramdoot Solutions
  • Cloud service providers (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) used for hosting, storage, or processing
  • Software vendors providing tools, libraries, or licensed products
  • Outsourced service providers (e.g., payroll, HR, legal, accounting)
  • Consultants and contractors with access to Ramdoot Solutions' systems or data
  • All Ramdoot Solutions employees involved in vendor selection, management, or oversight

3. Objectives

  • Identify and classify all vendors based on risk and criticality to business operations
  • Conduct appropriate due diligence before engaging vendors
  • Establish contractual requirements that protect Ramdoot Solutions' data and interests
  • Monitor vendor performance and security posture on an ongoing basis
  • Ensure orderly vendor termination with protection of organizational data
  • Maintain a comprehensive vendor inventory and risk register

6. Policy Statements

  1. All vendors shall be classified by risk tier (Critical, Standard, Low-Risk) before engagement and reassessed annually.
  2. Due diligence must be completed and documented before any vendor is granted access to Ramdoot Solutions' data or systems.
  3. All vendors handling confidential data shall execute an NDA and, where applicable, a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before commencing work.
  4. Critical vendors must provide evidence of their security posture through SOC 2 Type II reports, ISO 27001 certification, or equivalent independent assessments.
  5. Vendor contracts shall include security requirements, SLAs, incident notification obligations, audit rights, and termination provisions.
  6. Ongoing monitoring of vendor performance and security must be conducted at intervals proportionate to the vendor's risk classification.
  7. Vendor termination procedures shall ensure the return or destruction of all Ramdoot Solutions data and revocation of all access.
  8. A central vendor register must be maintained and kept current at all times.

7. Detailed Requirements

7.1 Vendor Classification

All vendors shall be classified into one of the following tiers based on their access to data, systems, and operational criticality:

7.2 Due Diligence

Before engaging any vendor classified as Critical or Standard, the following due diligence steps must be completed:

  1. Security Questionnaire: The vendor shall complete Ramdoot Solutions' vendor security questionnaire covering information security, data privacy, incident management, and business continuity.
  2. SOC/ISO Review: Critical vendors must provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate. If unavailable, an alternative security assessment shall be conducted.
  3. Financial Stability: For critical vendors, financial stability shall be assessed through publicly available financial statements, credit reports, or other appropriate means.
  4. References: At least two professional references should be obtained for critical vendor engagements.
  5. Regulatory Compliance: The vendor's compliance with applicable Indian regulations (IT Act 2000, DPDP Act 2023) shall be verified.

7.3 Risk Assessment

  • A formal vendor risk assessment shall be conducted using the likelihood-impact methodology defined in RS-POL-019 (Risk Management Policy).
  • The assessment must consider: data access level, system integration depth, geographic location, regulatory environment, business dependency, and historical incident record.
  • Risk assessment results shall be documented in the vendor risk register and presented to management for critical vendors.
  • Residual risks that exceed Ramdoot Solutions' risk appetite must be escalated to management for acceptance or mitigation.

7.5 Ongoing Monitoring

  • Critical vendors shall be reviewed at least annually, including a refresh of the vendor risk assessment and review of their latest SOC/ISO reports.
  • SLA performance must be tracked monthly for critical vendors and quarterly for standard vendors.
  • Any security incidents reported by or involving a vendor shall be investigated and documented in the incident management system.
  • Changes to vendor services, subprocessors, or data processing locations must be communicated to and approved by Ramdoot Solutions before implementation.
  • Annual vendor review meetings shall be conducted with all critical vendors.

7.6 SOC/ISO Report Collection

  • The Information Security Officer shall maintain a calendar of SOC report and ISO certificate expiration dates for all critical vendors.
  • Updated reports must be collected within 30 days of issuance.
  • Bridge letters shall be obtained if there is a gap between report periods.
  • Complementary User Entity Controls (CUECs) identified in vendor SOC reports must be reviewed and implemented by Ramdoot Solutions.

7.7 Vendor Termination

Upon vendor termination or contract expiration, the following steps must be completed:

  1. All Ramdoot Solutions data held by the vendor shall be returned in a usable format or securely destroyed, with written certification of destruction.
  2. All vendor access to Ramdoot Solutions' systems, networks, and facilities must be revoked on the termination date.
  3. Vendor-issued credentials and certificates shall be revoked or rotated.
  4. A final review must be conducted to ensure no residual data, access, or dependencies remain.
  5. The vendor register shall be updated to reflect the terminated status with the termination date and reason.
  6. Transition plans must be executed for critical vendors to ensure continuity of services.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally requested and approved. Each request shall include:

  • Justification for the exception and business need
  • Risk assessment of the exception
  • Compensating controls to mitigate identified risks
  • Time-bound duration (maximum 12 months before re-evaluation)

Exceptions for critical vendors shall require approval from [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • The Information Security Officer shall conduct an annual audit of vendor management practices against this policy.
  • Key metrics include: percentage of vendors with current risk assessments, SOC/ISO report coverage for critical vendors, SLA compliance rates, and overdue contract renewals.
  • The vendor register must be reviewed quarterly for accuracy and completeness.
  • Non-compliance findings shall be reported to management and tracked to remediation.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Immediate suspension of vendor access pending investigation
  • Contract review and potential termination for vendor-side violations
  • Disciplinary action for Ramdoot Solutions employees who engage vendors without following this policy
  • Formal incident reporting and root cause analysis
