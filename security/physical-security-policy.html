1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for physical and environmental security controls at Ramdoot Solutions' facilities. It ensures that the organization's premises, equipment, and information assets are protected against unauthorized physical access, damage, theft, and environmental hazards.

Physical security is a foundational element of Ramdoot Solutions' information security programme and supports compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC6.4) and NISP physical security requirements.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All Ramdoot Solutions office premises, including leased and co-working spaces
  • Server rooms, network closets, and data processing areas
  • Storage areas for physical documents, media, and equipment
  • All employees, contractors, visitors, and service personnel accessing Ramdoot Solutions facilities
  • All physical assets including servers, networking equipment, workstations, laptops, and mobile devices
  • Third-party data centre facilities used by Ramdoot Solutions

3. Objectives

  • Prevent unauthorized physical access to facilities and sensitive areas
  • Protect equipment and information assets from theft, damage, and environmental threats
  • Establish visitor management procedures that maintain security while supporting business operations
  • Implement environmental controls to protect against fire, water damage, power failure, and climate extremes
  • Enforce clean desk and secure area practices to reduce information exposure
  • Maintain auditable records of physical access and security events

6. Policy Statements

  1. All Ramdoot Solutions facilities shall have physical access controls proportionate to the sensitivity of the areas and assets they contain.
  2. Access to secure areas must be restricted to authorized personnel only, based on documented business need.
  3. All visitors shall be registered, issued temporary identification, and escorted at all times within secure areas.
  4. CCTV surveillance must be operational at all facility entry/exit points, server rooms, and other critical areas, with recordings retained for a minimum of 90 days.
  5. Environmental controls (fire suppression, HVAC, UPS) shall be maintained in server rooms and data processing areas.
  6. All employees must comply with the clean desk policy during and after working hours.
  7. Physical access rights shall be reviewed quarterly and revoked upon role change or termination.
  8. Physical security incidents must be reported immediately and investigated within 24 hours.

7. Detailed Requirements

7.1 Facility Access Control

7.1.1 Access Badges

  • All employees and long-term contractors shall be issued personalized access badges with their photograph, name, and role.
  • Access badges must be worn visibly at all times within the facility.
  • Lost or stolen badges must be reported to the Facilities Manager immediately and deactivated within 1 hour of report.
  • Badge sharing or tailgating (allowing others to pass through on one badge) is strictly prohibited.
  • Temporary badges for visitors shall be visually distinct from employee badges (e.g., different colour).

7.1.2 Biometric Access

  • Restricted zones (server rooms, network closets) shall require biometric authentication (fingerprint or facial recognition) in addition to badge access.
  • Biometric data must be stored encrypted and handled in compliance with RS-POL-020 (Privacy & Data Protection Policy).
  • Biometric templates shall be deleted within 30 days of an individual's access revocation.

7.2 Visitor Management

  1. Pre-registration: Visitors should be pre-registered by the hosting employee, providing the visitor's full name, organization, purpose of visit, and expected duration.
  2. Registration: All visitors must register at reception, present a valid government-issued photo ID, and sign the visitor register.
  3. Badge Issuance: Visitors shall receive a temporary visitor badge that clearly identifies them as a non-employee.
  4. Escort: Visitors must be escorted at all times within areas beyond the public zone. The hosting employee is responsible for the visitor.
  5. Checkout: Visitors shall return their temporary badge upon departure, and the visit end time must be recorded.
  6. Prohibited Areas: Visitors shall not be granted access to restricted zones (server rooms) without prior written approval from the Information Security Officer.

7.3 CCTV Surveillance

  • CCTV cameras shall be installed at: all facility entry/exit points, reception area, corridor intersections, server room entrance, and parking areas.
  • CCTV recordings must be retained for a minimum of 90 days.
  • Camera placement shall ensure coverage of critical areas while respecting privacy (no cameras in restrooms or prayer rooms).
  • Access to CCTV footage must be restricted to the Facilities Manager and Information Security Officer.
  • CCTV system health shall be checked weekly, with any camera failures resolved within 24 hours.
  • Signage indicating CCTV surveillance must be displayed at facility entrances.

7.5 Clean Desk Policy

  • Employees shall clear their desks of all sensitive documents and removable media at the end of each working day and whenever leaving their desk for extended periods.
  • Sensitive documents must be stored in locked drawers or cabinets when not in active use.
  • Whiteboards shall be erased after meetings if they contain sensitive information.
  • Printers and copiers must not be left unattended with sensitive documents in output trays.
  • Computer screens shall be locked when the employee steps away (automatic lock after 5 minutes of inactivity at minimum).
  • Random clean desk audits shall be conducted monthly by the Information Security Officer or designee.

7.6 Equipment Protection

  • All servers and critical network equipment shall be housed in locked racks within the restricted zone.
  • Laptops and portable devices must be secured with cable locks when left unattended in the office.
  • Equipment removal from facilities shall require written authorization from the employee's department head.
  • Decommissioned equipment must have all data securely wiped (NIST SP 800-88 guidelines) before disposal or reuse.
  • Equipment maintenance by external vendors shall be supervised by an authorized Ramdoot Solutions employee.
  • An equipment inventory must be maintained and reconciled quarterly.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally requested and approved. Each request shall include:

  • Justification for the exception
  • Risk assessment of the physical security implications
  • Compensating controls to be implemented
  • Duration of the exception (maximum 6 months before re-evaluation)

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • Physical access logs shall be reviewed monthly for anomalies (after-hours access, failed attempts, excessive visitor traffic).
  • Clean desk compliance audits must be conducted monthly with results documented.
  • CCTV system health and coverage shall be audited quarterly.
  • Environmental control systems must be tested per the schedules defined in Section 7.4.
  • Physical security access rights shall be reviewed quarterly and aligned with the current employee roster.
  • Annual physical security assessments shall be conducted, including penetration-style walk-through testing.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Verbal warning for first-time minor violations (e.g., clean desk non-compliance)
  • Written warning and mandatory retraining for repeated violations
  • Temporary access suspension pending investigation for serious violations (tailgating, unauthorized area access)
  • Formal disciplinary action, up to and including termination, for deliberate or egregious violations
  • Visitor banning and vendor contract review for third-party violations
