1. Purpose

This policy establishes the security requirements applicable to all employees and contractors throughout their engagement with Ramdoot Solutions — from pre-employment screening through the duration of their tenure. It ensures that personnel understand their information security obligations, are appropriately vetted, and are held accountable for protecting organizational assets.

People are the first line of defence in information security. This policy supports a security-aware culture and ensures compliance with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (CC1.4, CC1.5) and NISP human resource security requirements.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All prospective, current, and departing employees of Ramdoot Solutions
  • All contractors, consultants, and temporary workers engaged by Ramdoot Solutions
  • Interns and trainees with access to organizational systems or data
  • All phases of the employment lifecycle: recruitment, onboarding, ongoing employment, and role changes

Note. Termination and offboarding procedures are covered separately in RS-POL-024 (Termination & Offboarding Policy).

3. Objectives

  • Ensure that personnel with access to organizational information and systems are trustworthy and suitable
  • Establish clear security agreements and obligations before granting access
  • Maintain ongoing security awareness and competency across the workforce
  • Define clear responsibilities for reporting security events and incidents
  • Establish a fair and transparent disciplinary process for security violations
  • Ensure annual reaffirmation of security commitments

6. Policy Statements

  1. All prospective employees and contractors shall undergo pre-employment screening proportionate to their role and access level before being granted access to organizational systems or data.
  2. All personnel must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and acknowledge the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) before receiving access credentials.
  3. Security awareness training shall be mandatory for all personnel within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter.
  4. All employees must understand and fulfil their responsibility to report security incidents, policy violations, and suspicious activities.
  5. Violations of security policies shall be addressed through a formal, documented disciplinary process that is fair and proportionate.
  6. An annual security policy acknowledgement must be completed by all personnel, confirming their understanding and acceptance of their obligations.
  7. Security responsibilities shall be clearly documented in job descriptions and employment contracts.
  8. Contractors and third-party personnel must meet the same security standards as employees, with obligations codified in their engagement contracts.

7.2 Security Agreements

Before being granted access to any organizational systems or data, all personnel must execute the following agreements:

7.2.1 Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

  • The NDA shall cover all confidential and proprietary information encountered during the engagement.
  • The NDA must specify that obligations survive termination of employment/engagement for a period of [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] years.
  • The NDA shall include provisions for intellectual property assignment where applicable.
  • Separate NDAs may be required for specific projects or client engagements with additional confidentiality requirements.

7.2.2 Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) Acknowledgement

  • All personnel must read and acknowledge the AUP, which covers authorized use of organizational IT resources, email, internet, and data.
  • The AUP acknowledgement shall be documented with the employee's signature (physical or electronic) and date.
  • Updated AUPs must be re-acknowledged within 30 days of any material changes.

7.2.3 Employment Contract Security Clauses

Employment contracts shall include clauses covering:

  • Information security responsibilities specific to the role
  • Obligation to comply with all organizational security policies
  • Consequences of security policy violations
  • Obligations that continue after termination of employment

7.3.2 Training Topics

Security awareness training shall cover, at minimum:

  • Password security and multi-factor authentication
  • Phishing, social engineering, and vishing recognition
  • Data classification and handling procedures
  • Clean desk and physical security practices
  • Remote work security requirements
  • Acceptable use of IT resources
  • Incident reporting procedures
  • Privacy and data protection obligations (DPDP Act 2023)
  • Secure software development practices (for technical roles)

7.3.3 Training Completion Tracking

  • Training completion shall be tracked centrally, with records maintained for a minimum of 3 years.
  • Employees who do not complete mandatory training within the specified timeframe shall have their system access suspended until training is completed.
  • Training completion rates must be reported to management quarterly (target: 100% compliance).

7.4 Reporting Responsibilities

  • All employees shall report suspected security incidents, policy violations, or vulnerabilities immediately to the Information Security Officer via [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].
  • Reports may be made anonymously through the organization's confidential reporting mechanism.
  • Employees must not attempt to investigate incidents themselves — investigation is the responsibility of the Information Security Officer.
  • No retaliation shall be taken against employees who report security concerns in good faith.
  • Failure to report a known security incident shall itself be treated as a policy violation.

7.5 Disciplinary Process

Security policy violations shall be addressed through the following progressive disciplinary process:

LevelViolation TypeActionAuthority
Level 1 — AdvisoryFirst-time minor violation (e.g., unlocked screen, training overdue)Verbal counselling; documented discussion; mandatory retrainingDirect Manager + ISO
Level 2 — WarningRepeated minor violation or first-time moderate violation (e.g., unauthorized software, sharing credentials)Written warning; mandatory retraining; enhanced monitoring for 90 daysHR + ISO + Department Head
Level 3 — SuspensionSerious violation (e.g., deliberate policy circumvention, data mishandling)Temporary suspension of access and/or employment; formal investigationHR + ISO + Senior Management
Level 4 — TerminationEgregious violation (e.g., data theft, wilful destruction, fraud)Termination of employment; legal action as appropriateSenior Management + Legal

Due Process. All disciplinary actions shall follow Ramdoot Solutions' HR disciplinary procedures, ensuring the employee has the opportunity to respond before any action is taken.

7.6 Annual Acknowledgement

  • All employees and active contractors must complete an annual security policy acknowledgement, confirming that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with the organization's security policies.
  • The acknowledgement shall be distributed annually during [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] and must be completed within 30 days.
  • The acknowledgement must cover: Information Security Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Data Protection Policy, Remote Work Policy (if applicable), and any role-specific security requirements.
  • Employees who fail to complete the acknowledgement within the specified period shall have their system access suspended until completion.
  • Acknowledgement records must be retained for audit purposes for a minimum of 3 years.

8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally requested and approved. Each request shall include:

  • Justification and business rationale
  • Risk assessment of the exception
  • Compensating controls
  • Time-bound duration (maximum 12 months)

Exceptions shall be approved by the Information Security Officer and [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS].

9. Monitoring & Compliance

  • HR and the Information Security Officer shall jointly audit compliance with pre-employment screening requirements quarterly.
  • Security training completion rates must be reported to management quarterly.
  • Annual acknowledgement completion rates shall be tracked, with non-compliant individuals reported to their managers.
  • NDA and AUP execution records must be auditable for 100% of active personnel.
  • The disciplinary process shall be reviewed annually for consistency and fairness.

10. Violations & Enforcement

Violations of this policy are addressed through the disciplinary process defined in Section 7.5. Additionally:

  • Managers who fail to ensure their team members complete mandatory training or acknowledgements may themselves be subject to disciplinary action.
  • HR personnel who fail to complete required screening for new hires shall be reported to senior management.
  • All disciplinary actions related to security violations shall be documented and retained in the personnel file.
