2. Purpose

This Asset Management Policy establishes requirements for the identification, classification, ownership, tracking, and lifecycle management of all information assets owned or managed by Ramdoot Solutions. Effective asset management is foundational to protecting information assets and ensuring that security controls are appropriately applied throughout the asset lifecycle.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All hardware assets — servers, workstations, laptops, mobile devices, networking equipment, peripherals, and removable media.
  • All software assets — operating systems, applications, databases, middleware, development tools, and licenses.
  • All information/data assets — databases, files, documents, intellectual property, and records.
  • All cloud-based assets — virtual machines, storage, SaaS subscriptions, and cloud infrastructure components.
  • All employees, contractors, and third parties responsible for managing or using Ramdoot Solutions assets.

4. Objectives

  1. Maintain a comprehensive, accurate inventory of all information assets.
  2. Assign clear ownership and accountability for every asset.
  3. Classify assets according to their value, sensitivity, and criticality to enable proportionate protection.
  4. Manage assets through their complete lifecycle — from procurement to secure disposal.
  5. Support compliance with regulatory requirements and contractual obligations related to asset protection.
  6. Maintain a Configuration Management Database (CMDB) or equivalent asset register as the authoritative source of truth.

6. Roles and Responsibilities

6.1 Information Security Officer

The ISO shall oversee asset classification standards, ensure alignment of asset management practices with security requirements, and review the asset inventory during annual policy reviews.

6.2 IT Department / Asset Custodians

IT shall maintain the CMDB, process procurement and disposal requests, perform regular inventory reconciliation, manage software licenses, and enforce asset security configurations.

6.3 Asset Owners

Asset owners shall classify assets under their responsibility, ensure appropriate protection controls are applied, authorize access to their assets, and initiate disposal procedures when assets reach end of life.

6.4 Finance / Procurement

Finance shall collaborate with IT to track asset acquisitions, warranty periods, and depreciation. Procurement shall ensure all new assets are registered in the CMDB upon receipt.

6.5 All Employees

Employees shall safeguard assets assigned to them, report loss or damage immediately, return assets upon termination, and shall not install unauthorized hardware or software on company assets.

7. Policy Statements

7.1 Asset Inventory

Ramdoot Solutions shall maintain a comprehensive inventory of all information assets in a centralized CMDB or asset register. The inventory shall include, at minimum, the following attributes for each asset: asset ID, description, type (hardware/software/data), owner, custodian, location, classification level, acquisition date, warranty/license expiry, and status.

7.2 Asset Ownership

Every information asset shall have a designated owner. Asset ownership shall be assigned at the time of procurement or creation. Ownership shall be reviewed and updated when organizational changes occur. The asset owner is accountable for ensuring the asset is adequately protected according to its classification.

7.3 Asset Classification

All assets shall be classified according to the Data Classification Policy (RS-POL-006) based on the sensitivity and criticality of the information they store or process. The classification level shall determine the minimum security controls applied to the asset.

7.4 Procurement

All IT asset procurement must follow the approved procurement process. Security requirements shall be evaluated during the procurement phase. Newly acquired assets must be registered in the CMDB before deployment. Software procurement shall include verification of licensing terms and compliance requirements.

7.5 Deployment and Configuration

Assets shall be configured in accordance with approved security baselines before deployment. Default passwords, unnecessary services, and test accounts shall be removed or disabled. The deployment configuration shall be documented in the CMDB.

7.6 Maintenance and Patching

Assets shall be maintained and patched in accordance with vendor recommendations and Ramdoot Solutions vulnerability management procedures. End-of-life assets that can no longer receive security updates shall be scheduled for replacement.

7.7 Software License Management

Ramdoot Solutions shall maintain accurate records of all software licenses. The organization must not use software in violation of licensing agreements. License compliance audits shall be conducted at least annually. Unauthorized or unlicensed software must be removed immediately upon discovery.

7.8 Hardware Tracking

All hardware assets shall be physically labeled with an asset tag corresponding to the CMDB entry. The physical location of hardware assets shall be tracked and updated when assets are moved. Mobile devices and laptops shall be tracked using endpoint management tools.

7.9 Disposal and Decommissioning

Assets being retired or disposed of shall undergo secure data sanitization in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 guidelines or equivalent standards, as referenced in the Data Retention Policy (RS-POL-007). Disposal methods shall be appropriate to the asset's classification level. Certificates of destruction shall be obtained and retained for assets containing Confidential or Restricted data. The asset status in the CMDB shall be updated to reflect disposal.

8. Detailed Requirements

8.1 Inventory Reconciliation

A physical inventory reconciliation shall be performed at least annually to verify the CMDB against actual assets. Discrepancies (missing, unregistered, or mislocated assets) shall be investigated and resolved within [TO BE COMPLETED BY RAMDOOT SOLUTIONS] business days. Automated discovery tools shall supplement manual reconciliation where feasible.

8.2 Cloud Asset Management

Cloud resources (virtual machines, databases, storage accounts, SaaS subscriptions) shall be inventoried in the CMDB alongside physical assets. Cloud asset creation shall follow approved provisioning processes. Unused or orphaned cloud resources shall be identified and decommissioned monthly.

8.3 Return of Assets

Upon termination of employment or contract, all Ramdoot Solutions assets in the individual's possession shall be returned. The return process shall be coordinated between HR, IT, and the departing individual's manager. Asset return shall be verified and documented before final clearance is granted.

8.4 Asset Risk Assessment

Critical assets shall undergo risk assessments as part of the annual security risk assessment process. Assets approaching end-of-life shall be specifically assessed for risks related to unpatched vulnerabilities.

8.5 CMDB Data Quality

The CMDB shall be treated as a controlled record. Updates shall be logged with timestamps and responsible personnel. Data quality reviews shall be conducted quarterly to ensure accuracy and completeness.

9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written justification, a risk assessment, and approval from the Information Security Officer. Exceptions shall be documented, time-bound (maximum 12 months), and reviewed quarterly. Compensating controls must be identified for any approved exception.

10. Monitoring and Compliance

  • Annual asset inventory reconciliation with documented results.
  • Quarterly software license compliance audits.
  • Monthly reviews of cloud resource utilization and orphaned resources.
  • Tracking of asset lifecycle milestones (warranty expiry, EOL dates, disposal).
  • Internal audits shall include verification of CMDB accuracy and asset management processes.

11. Violations and Enforcement

Failure to register assets, unauthorized disposal of assets, installation of unauthorized hardware or software, or failure to return assets upon termination constitutes a policy violation. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Financial liability may apply for lost or damaged assets due to negligence.

12. Records and Evidence

  • CMDB / asset register (maintained as a living document).
  • Asset procurement and disposal records.
  • Certificates of destruction for disposed assets.
  • Inventory reconciliation reports.
  • Software license records and compliance audit reports.
  • Asset return acknowledgment forms.
  • Exception request forms and approvals.

Records shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention Policy (RS-POL-007).

15. Review Cycle

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually, or sooner upon:

  • Significant changes to the IT asset landscape (e.g., cloud migration, major procurement).
  • Loss or theft of assets.
  • Audit findings related to asset management.
  • Changes in regulatory or compliance requirements.
