1. Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive list of all evidence items required for Ramdoot Solutions' compliance readiness across SOC 2, SOC 1, and NISP frameworks. Each entry specifies the exact evidence needed, the associated control reference, the responsible owner, the time period required, and current collection status.

Responsible owners should use this list to identify, gather, and submit evidence items to the compliance team. All evidence must be current, complete, and verifiable.

2. Instructions for Evidence Owners

  1. Locate your assigned evidence items (filter by "Responsible Owner" column).
  2. Gather the requested evidence for the specified time period.
  3. Ensure screenshots include timestamps, system names, and are not cropped or redacted unless specifically required.
  4. Submit evidence to the compliance team in the designated shared folder with the Evidence ID as the filename prefix.
  5. Evidence marked "Last 12 months" requires samples or records covering the full trailing 12-month period.
  6. Evidence marked "Point-in-time" requires a current-state snapshot as of the collection date.
