1. Purpose

This document maps evidence items across multiple compliance frameworks to demonstrate how a single piece of evidence satisfies requirements from SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria, SOC 1 Control Objectives, and NISP sections. This cross-referencing reduces duplicate evidence collection efforts, ensures comprehensive coverage, and identifies gaps where additional evidence may be needed.

Each row identifies the applicable framework references, supporting Ramdoot Solutions policy, the specific evidence required, and the evidence ID from the Evidence Request List (RS-EV-REQ-001).

5. Identified Gaps

NISP §7 — Physical & Environmental Security. No evidence items have been mapped to physical and environmental security controls. Ramdoot Solutions must determine whether physical security evidence is required based on its operating model (e.g., office-based, remote, co-located data center) and add corresponding evidence requests.

SOC1-CO3 — Segregation of Duties. Only one evidence item (RS-EVREQ-020, privileged access list) currently maps to segregation of duties. Additional evidence demonstrating separation of development, operations, and security functions may be required depending on SOC 1 scope determination.

All gaps should be reviewed during the compliance readiness assessment and additional evidence requests created as needed in the Evidence Request List.
