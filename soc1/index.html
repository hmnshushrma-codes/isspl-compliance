SOC 1 Documentation Index

About SOC 1. SOC 1 (System and Organization Controls 1) reports address controls at a service organization relevant to user entities' Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR). These are internal readiness documents prepared by Ramdoot Solutions and do not constitute a SOC 1 Type I or Type II attestation report.

Applicability Notice. SOC 1 applicability requires confirmation based on the services provided to customers and their relationship to Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR). The documents below are prepared as a readiness exercise pending that determination.
