1. Purpose

This document assesses Ramdoot Solutions' readiness for a SOC 1 examination covering controls that are relevant to customer entities' Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR). The purpose is to:

  • Evaluate the current state of internal controls that may impact the financial reporting of Ramdoot Solutions' customers;
  • Identify control objectives relevant to ICFR and assess whether corresponding control activities are designed, documented, and operating effectively;
  • Highlight gaps, exceptions, and remediation actions necessary before engaging an independent service auditor;
  • Establish a baseline for ongoing SOC 1 compliance governance.

Note: This is an internal readiness document. No SOC 1 Type I or Type II report has been issued for Ramdoot Solutions. This assessment is intended to prepare the organisation for a potential future engagement with an independent service auditor under SSAE 18 (AT-C Section 320) or ISAE 3402.

3. SOC 1 Type I vs Type II

SOC 1 reports are issued under SSAE 18 (United States) or ISAE 3402 (international). Two types exist:

AttributeSOC 1 Type ISOC 1 Type II
Focus Design suitability of controls at a specific point in time Design suitability and operating effectiveness of controls over a period of time
Timeframe As of a specified date (point-in-time) Throughout a specified period (typically 6 or 12 months)
Auditor Testing Evaluates whether controls are suitably designed to achieve the stated control objectives Evaluates design and tests whether controls operated effectively during the review period
Report Contents Management's description, auditor's opinion on design, description of controls Management's description, auditor's opinion on design and operating effectiveness, description of tests and results
Common Use Initial SOC 1 engagement; baseline assessment Ongoing annual engagement; required by most user entities and their auditors

Current Status: Neither a SOC 1 Type I nor a SOC 1 Type II report has been issued for Ramdoot Solutions. This readiness assessment is an internal exercise to prepare for a potential future SOC 1 engagement.

8. Change Management Controls

Change management controls ensure that modifications to in-scope systems are authorised, tested, and documented before deployment to production.

Control AreaExpected ControlStatus

13. Complementary User Entity Controls (CUECs)

CUECs are controls that user entities (customers) are expected to implement at their end for the overall control objectives to be achieved. These are disclosed in a SOC 1 report to indicate shared responsibility.

Note: CUECs must be clearly communicated to user entities so their auditors can evaluate the complete control environment.

RefCUEC DescriptionRelated Control Objective
CUEC-01 User entities are responsible for managing the security and confidentiality of their own authentication credentials (passwords, API keys, tokens) used to access Ramdoot Solutions' systems. CO-01 (Logical Access)
CUEC-02 User entities are responsible for promptly notifying Ramdoot Solutions when personnel who have been granted access are terminated or transferred. CO-01 (Logical Access)
CUEC-03 User entities are responsible for reviewing and validating the accuracy and completeness of output received from Ramdoot Solutions before relying upon it for financial reporting purposes. CO-03 (Transaction Processing)
CUEC-04 User entities are responsible for ensuring the accuracy and completeness of data submitted to Ramdoot Solutions for processing. CO-03 (Transaction Processing)

17. Management Responsibilities

Under SSAE 18 / ISAE 3402, management of the service organisation bears the following responsibilities:

17.1 Management's Assertion

Management is required to provide a written assertion that:

  1. The description of the service organisation's system fairly presents the system as designed and implemented throughout the specified period (Type II) or as of a specified date (Type I);
  2. The controls related to the control objectives stated in the description were suitably designed (Type I) or suitably designed and operating effectively (Type II) throughout the specified period;
  3. The criteria used to make the assertion are the criteria set forth in the applicable trust services criteria or relevant framework.
